Q1: Can I carry forward unused annual leave?
Source: policy_hr_leave.txt
[policy_hr_leave.txt, section 2.6] Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused
    annual leave days to the following calendar year. Any days above 5 are forfeited on
    31 December.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q2: Can I install Slack on my work laptop?
Source: policy_it_acceptable_use.txt
[policy_it_acceptable_use.txt, section 2.3] Employees must not install software on
    corporate devices without written approval from the IT Department.
[policy_it_acceptable_use.txt, section 2.6] Corporate devices must have the CMC endpoint
    security agent installed and active at all times. Disabling or circumventing this
    agent is a disciplinary offence.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q3: What is the home office equipment allowance?
Source: policy_finance_reimbursement.txt
[policy_finance_reimbursement.txt, section 3.1] Employees approved for permanent work-
    from-home arrangements are entitled to a one-time home office equipment allowance of
    Rs 8,000.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q4: Can I use my personal phone to access work files when working from home?
Source: policy_it_acceptable_use.txt
[policy_it_acceptable_use.txt, section 3.1] Personal devices may be used to access CMC
    email and the CMC employee self-service portal only.
[policy_it_acceptable_use.txt, section 3.2] Personal devices must not be used to access,
    store, or transmit classified or sensitive CMC data.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q5: What is the company view on flexible working culture?
This question is not covered in the available policy documents

Please contact the relevant department (HR, IT or Finance) for guidance.

Q6: Can I claim DA and meal receipts on the same day?
Source: policy_finance_reimbursement.txt
[policy_finance_reimbursement.txt, section 2.5] Daily allowance (DA) for outstation
    travel is Rs 750 per day. DA covers meals and incidentals. No separate meal receipts
    are required if DA is claimed.
[policy_finance_reimbursement.txt, section 2.6] If actual meal expenses are claimed
    instead of DA, receipts are mandatory and the combined meal claim must not exceed Rs
    750 per day. DA and meal receipts cannot be claimed simultaneously for the same day.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q7: Who approves leave without pay?
Source: policy_hr_leave.txt
[policy_hr_leave.txt, section 5.1] An employee may apply for Leave Without Pay only
    after exhausting all applicable paid leave entitlements.
[policy_hr_leave.txt, section 5.2] LWP requires approval from the Department Head and
    the HR Director. Manager approval alone is not sufficient.
[policy_hr_leave.txt, section 5.3] LWP exceeding 30 continuous days requires approval
    from the Municipal Commissioner.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
