Your question:  Can I claim DA and meal receipts on the same day?

[policy_finance_reimbursement.txt, Section 2.1]
2.1 Local travel (within city limits) is reimbursable at actual
    cost for public transport or at Rs 4 per km for personal
    vehicle use. Receipts are required for all claims above Rs 200.

[policy_finance_reimbursement.txt, Section 2.5]
2.5 Daily allowance (DA) for outstation travel is Rs 750 per day.
    DA covers meals and incidentals. No separate meal receipts
    are required if DA is claimed.

[policy_finance_reimbursement.txt, Section 2.6]
2.6 If actual meal expenses are claimed instead of DA, receipts
    are mandatory and the combined meal claim must not exceed
    Rs 750 per day. DA and meal receipts cannot be claimed
    simultaneously for the same day.
