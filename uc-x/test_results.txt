Q: Can I carry forward unused annual leave?
A: Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year. Any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December. [policy_hr_leave.txt, Section 2.6]

Q: Can I install Slack on my work laptop?
A: The installation of unauthorized software is prohibited. All software requests must be submitted to the IT Department in writing for security review and approval. [policy_it_acceptable_use.txt, Section 2.3]

Q: What is the home office equipment allowance?
A: A one-time home office setup allowance of Rs 8,000 is available for employees on permanent work-from-home contracts. [policy_finance_reimbursement.txt, Section 3.1]

Q: Can I use my personal phone for work files from home?
A: Personal devices may be used to access CMC email and the employee self-service portal only. Accessing other work files is not mentioned as permitted. [policy_it_acceptable_use.txt, Section 3.1]
