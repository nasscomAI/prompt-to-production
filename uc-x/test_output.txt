------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Can I carry forward unused annual leave?
------------------------------------------------------------
Source: policy_hr_leave.txt, Section 2.6

2. ANNUAL LEAVE
2.6 Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave
    days to the following calendar year. Any days above 5 are
    forfeited on 31 December.

------------------------------------------------------------
Q2: Can I install Slack on my work laptop?
------------------------------------------------------------
Source: policy_it_acceptable_use.txt, Section 2.3

2. CORPORATE DEVICES
2.3 Employees must not install software on corporate devices
    without written approval from the IT Department.

------------------------------------------------------------
Q3: What is the home office equipment allowance?
------------------------------------------------------------
Source: policy_finance_reimbursement.txt, Section 3.1

3. WORK FROM HOME EQUIPMENT
3.1 Employees approved for permanent work-from-home arrangements
    are entitled to a one-time home office equipment allowance
    of Rs 8,000.

------------------------------------------------------------
Q4: Can I use my personal phone to access work files when working from home?
------------------------------------------------------------
Source: policy_it_acceptable_use.txt, Section 2.1

2. CORPORATE DEVICES
2.1 Corporate devices (laptops, desktops, mobile phones issued by
    CMC) must be used primarily for official work purposes.

Q6: Can I claim DA and meal receipts on the same day?
------------------------------------------------------------
Source: policy_finance_reimbursement.txt, Section 2.6

2. TRAVEL REIMBURSEMENT
2.6 If actual meal expenses are claimed instead of DA, receipts
    are mandatory and the combined meal claim must not exceed
    Rs 750 per day. DA and meal receipts cannot be claimed
    simultaneously for the same day.

------------------------------------------------------------
Q7: Who approves leave without pay?
------------------------------------------------------------
Source: policy_hr_leave.txt, Section 5.2

5. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)
5.2 LWP requires approval from the Department Head and the
    HR Director. Manager approval alone is not sufficient.
