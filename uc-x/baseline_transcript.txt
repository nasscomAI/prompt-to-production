BASELINE TRANSCRIPT — naive prompt: "Answer questions about company policy."
Recorded before any enforcement was added.

Q1: Can I carry forward unused annual leave?
A1: Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year. Any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December. Each permanent employee is entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues at 1.5 days per month from the date of joining.

Q2: Can I install Slack on my work laptop?
A2: While not explicitly covered, it is generally understood that Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 48 hours of returning to work. If an employee is required to work on a public holiday, they are entitled to one compensatory off day, to be taken within 60 days of the holiday worked. Corporate devices (laptops, desktops, mobile phones issued by CMC) must be used primarily for official work purposes.

Q3: What is the home office equipment allowance?
A3: Employees approved for permanent work-from-home arrangements are entitled to a one-time home office equipment allowance of Rs 8,000. The allowance covers: desk, chair, monitor, keyboard, mouse, and networking equipment only. The allowance does not cover: personal computers, laptops, smartphones, printers, or air conditioning equipment.

Q4: Can I use my personal phone to access work files when working from home?
A4: Personal use of corporate devices is permitted in moderation, provided it does not interfere with work duties or consume excessive bandwidth. Personal devices may be used to access CMC email and the CMC employee self-service portal only. Personal devices must not be used to access, store, or transmit classified or sensitive CMC data.

Q5: What is the company view on flexible working culture?
A5: While not explicitly covered, it is generally understood that Leave-related grievances must be raised with the HR Department within 10 working days of the disputed decision. Grievances raised after 10 working days will not be considered unless exceptional circumstances are demonstrated in writing. Claims are processed within 15 working days of submission.

Q6: Can I claim DA and meal receipts on the same day?
A6: If actual meal expenses are claimed instead of DA, receipts are mandatory and the combined meal claim must not exceed Rs 750 per day. DA and meal receipts cannot be claimed simultaneously for the same day. Daily allowance (DA) for outstation travel is Rs 750 per day. DA covers meals and incidentals. No separate meal receipts are required if DA is claimed. If an employee is required to work on a public holiday, they are entitled to one compensatory off day, to be taken within 60 days of the holiday worked.

Q7: Who approves leave without pay?
A7: An employee may apply for Leave Without Pay only after exhausting all applicable paid leave entitlements. This policy governs all leave entitlements for permanent and contractual employees of the City Municipal Corporation (CMC). Each permanent employee is entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
