Q: Can I carry forward unused annual leave?
A: Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year, and any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December (policy_hr_leave.txt §2.6). Carry-forward days must be used within January–March of the following year or they are forfeited (policy_hr_leave.txt §2.7).

Q: Can I install Slack on my work laptop?
A: Employees must not install software on corporate devices without written approval from the IT Department (policy_it_acceptable_use.txt §2.3).

Q: What is the home office equipment allowance?
A: Employees approved for permanent work-from-home arrangements are entitled to a one-time home office equipment allowance of Rs 8,000 (policy_finance_reimbursement.txt §3.1). Employees on temporary or partial work-from-home arrangements are not eligible for this allowance (policy_finance_reimbursement.txt §3.5).

Q: Can I use my personal phone for work files from home?
A: No. Personal devices may be used to access CMC email and the CMC employee self-service portal only (policy_it_acceptable_use.txt §3.1). Personal devices must not be used to access, store, or transmit classified or sensitive CMC data (policy_it_acceptable_use.txt §3.2).

Q: What is the company view on flexible working culture?
A: This question is not covered in the available policy documents
(policy_hr_leave.txt, policy_it_acceptable_use.txt, policy_finance_reimbursement.txt).
Please contact Human Resources Department for guidance.

Q: Can I claim DA and meal receipts on the same day?
A: No. If actual meal expenses are claimed instead of DA, receipts are mandatory and the combined meal claim must not exceed Rs 750 per day, and DA and meal receipts cannot be claimed simultaneously for the same day (policy_finance_reimbursement.txt §2.6).

Q: Who approves leave without pay?
A: Leave Without Pay requires approval from the Department Head and the HR Director, and manager approval alone is not sufficient (policy_hr_leave.txt §5.2).
