Question 1: Can I carry forward unused annual leave?
Answer: According to policy_hr_leave.txt Section 2.6, Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year. Any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December.
----------------------------------------
Question 2: Can I install Slack on my work laptop?
Answer: According to policy_it_acceptable_use.txt Section 2.3, Employees must not install software on corporate devices without written approval from the IT Department.
----------------------------------------
Question 3: What is the home office equipment allowance?
Answer: According to policy_finance_reimbursement.txt Section 3.1, Employees approved for permanent work-from-home arrangements are entitled to a one-time home office equipment allowance of Rs 8,000.
----------------------------------------
Question 4: Can I use my personal phone to access work files when working from home?
Answer: According to policy_it_acceptable_use.txt Section 3.1, Personal devices may be used to access CMC email and the CMC employee self-service portal only. Also, Section 3.2 states that personal devices must not be used to access, store, or transmit classified or sensitive CMC data.

Question 6: Can I claim DA and meal receipts on the same day?
Answer: According to policy_finance_reimbursement.txt Section 2.6, If actual meal expenses are claimed instead of DA, receipts are mandatory and the combined meal claim must not exceed Rs 750 per day. DA and meal receipts cannot be claimed simultaneously for the same day.
----------------------------------------
Question 7: Who approves leave without pay?
Answer: According to policy_hr_leave.txt Section 5.2, LWP requires approval from the Department Head and the HR Director. Manager approval alone is not sufficient.
