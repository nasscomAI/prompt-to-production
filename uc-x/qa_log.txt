Q: Can I carry forward unused annual leave?
A: policy_hr_leave.txt, Section 2.6: Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year. Any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December.
Q: Can I install Slack on my work laptop?
A: policy_it_acceptable_use.txt, Section 2.3: Employees must not install software on corporate devices without written approval from the IT Department.
Q: What is the home office equipment allowance?
A: policy_finance_reimbursement.txt, Section 3.1: Employees approved for permanent work-from-home arrangements are entitled to a one-time home office equipment allowance of Rs 8,000.
Q: Can I use my personal phone for work files from home?
A: policy_it_acceptable_use.txt, Section 3.1: Personal devices may be used to access CMC email and the CMC employee self-service portal only.
Q: What is the company view on flexible working culture?
A: This question is not covered in the available policy documents (policy_hr_leave.txt, policy_it_acceptable_use.txt, policy_finance_reimbursement.txt). Please contact [relevant team] for guidance.
Q: Can I claim DA and meal receipts on the same day?
A: policy_finance_reimbursement.txt, Section 2.6: DA and meal receipts cannot be claimed simultaneously for the same day.
Q: Who approves leave without pay?
A: policy_hr_leave.txt, Section 5.2: LWP requires approval from the Department Head and the HR Director. Manager approval alone is not sufficient.
