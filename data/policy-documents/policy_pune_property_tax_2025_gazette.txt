PUNE MUNICIPAL CORPORATION (PMC) - OFFICIAL GAZETTE NOTIFICATION (2025)
[EFFECTIVE DATE: APRIL 1, 2025 - CURRENT OPERATIONAL POLICY]

1. Legislative Overrule & Amendments
1.1 This notification supersedes all prior PMC property tax guidelines, including the Property Tax Rules (2023), for assessments starting fiscal year 2025-2026.

2. Scope and Jurisdiction
2.1 Applies exclusively to Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) properties. Properties located in PCMC, PMRDA, or Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation are strictly excluded.

3. Concessions & Amended Rebates (2025)
3.1 General Tax Rebate: The 40% rebate is retained exclusively for verified self-occupied residences with PT-3 declaration on file.
3.2 Online Payment Rebate (Amended): Effective April 1, 2025, digital payments completed before May 31 receive a 5% rebate, strictly capped at a maximum of Rs. 1,500 per property PIN. The previous uncapped 10% scheme is discontinued.

4. Solar & Water Harvesting Incentive
4.1 Properties with functional rainwater harvesting and solar panels receive an additional 5% rebate on general tax upon physical audit verification.
