AHMEDABAD MUNICIPAL CORPORATION (AMC) - PROPERTY TAX BYLAWS (2025)
[EFFECTIVE DATE: JANUARY 1, 2025 - CURRENT OPERATIONAL POLICY]

1. Scope and Jurisdiction
1.1 These bylaws apply strictly to properties situated within the jurisdictional limits of Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation (AMC), Gujarat.

2. Green Building Incentive (AMC Specific)
2.1 Certified GRIHA/IGBC Green Buildings within AMC limits are entitled to an 8% flat rebate on general tax.

3. Early Bird Discount
3.1 Advance tax payments made in the first quarter of the fiscal year receive a 10% early bird rebate (capped at Rs. 3,000).

4. Refusal & Cross-Jurisdiction Clause
4.1 AMC tax rebates cannot be combined with or applied to properties located outside Ahmedabad (such as Pune, Surat, or Mumbai).
