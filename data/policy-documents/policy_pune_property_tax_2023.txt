PUNE MUNICIPAL CORPORATION (PMC) - PROPERTY TAX RULES (2023)
[HISTORICAL DOCUMENT - SUPERSEDED FOR FISCAL YEARS AFTER MARCH 2025]

1. Scope and Jurisdiction
1.1 These rules apply exclusively to taxable residential and commercial properties within the municipal limits of Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC).

2. General Assessment
2.1 Annual Ratable Value (ARV) is calculated based on built-up area and zonal base rate.

3. Concessions & Rebates (Historical)
3.1 General Tax Rebate: A 40% rebate on general municipal tax is applicable for self-occupied residential properties registered prior to 2019.
3.2 Online Payment Rebate: A flat 10% rebate on total property tax is granted for all digital payments completed before May 31 of the assessment year, with no maximum ceiling.

4. Penalty & Interest
4.1 Late payment of property tax after June 30 shall incur a penalty interest of 2% per month.
