UC-0B Policy Summary Skills
Skill 1: Clause Preservation
Read the complete policy and preserve all 10 required clauses in the summary.
Skill 2: Requirement Extraction
For each clause, identify:
Clause number
Core obligation
Binding verb
Deadline or time limit
Condition or exception
Consequence, when applicable
Skill 3: Meaning Preservation
Keep the original strength of each requirement. Do not weaken "must" or "requires" into optional language.
Skill 4: Approval Accuracy
Preserve every required approver. For Clause 5.2, state that LWP requires approval from both the Department Head AND the HR Director.
Skill 5: Final Verification
Before producing the final summary, check that:
All 10 clauses are represented.
No deadlines or limits are missing.
No approval requirement is omitted.
No new rule has been invented.
The summary remains concise and faithful to the source.
