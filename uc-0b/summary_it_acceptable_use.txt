POLICY SUMMARY — clause-preserving (UC-0B)

Section 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE
  1.1: This policy governs acceptable use of all IT systems, devices, networks, and data owned or managed by the City Municipal Corporation (CMC).
  1.2: This policy applies to all permanent employees, contractual staff, and third-party vendors with CMC system access.
  1.3: Use of CMC IT resources implies acceptance of this policy.

Section 2: CORPORATE DEVICES
  2.1 [BINDING]: Corporate devices (laptops, desktops, mobile phones issued by CMC) must be used primarily for official work purposes.
  2.2: Personal use of corporate devices is permitted in moderation, provided it does not interfere with work duties or consume excessive bandwidth.
  2.3 [BINDING]: Employees must not install software on corporate devices without written approval from the IT Department.
  2.4 [BINDING]: Software approved for installation must be sourced from the CMC-approved software catalogue only.
  2.5 [BINDING]: Corporate devices must not be used to access gambling, adult content, or any site classified as harmful by the IT Department.
  2.6 [VERBATIM]: "Corporate devices must have the CMC endpoint security agent installed and active at all times. Disabling or circumventing this agent is a disciplinary offence."

Section 3: PERSONAL DEVICES (BYOD)
  3.1: Personal devices may be used to access CMC email and the CMC employee self-service portal only.
  3.2 [BINDING]: Personal devices must not be used to access, store, or transmit classified or sensitive CMC data.
  3.3 [BINDING]: Personal devices must not be connected to the CMC internal network (wired or wireless). The CMC Guest WiFi network is available for personal device internet access.
  3.4 [BINDING]: Employees using personal devices for CMC email must enable device-level PIN or biometric lock.
  3.5 [BINDING]: If a personal device containing CMC email is lost or stolen, the employee must report it to the IT helpdesk within 4 hours so that a remote wipe of CMC data can be performed.

Section 4: PASSWORDS AND ACCESS CONTROL
  4.1 [BINDING]: Employees must not share their CMC system passwords with any other person, including IT staff.
  4.2 [BINDING]: IT staff will never ask for your password. Any request for your password should be reported to the IT Security team.
  4.3 [BINDING]: Passwords must be changed every 90 days as prompted by the system.
  4.4: Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all remote access to CMC systems.

Section 5: DATA HANDLING
  5.1 [BINDING]: CMC data classified as Confidential or Restricted must not be stored on personal devices, personal cloud storage, or any system not approved by the IT Department.
  5.2 [BINDING]: Employees must not forward CMC email containing Confidential data to personal email accounts.
  5.3 [BINDING]: Printing of Confidential documents must use the secure print function. Documents must not be left unattended at printers.

Section 6: INTERNET AND EMAIL USE
  6.1: Internet use on CMC systems is monitored and logged.
  6.2 [BINDING]: Employees must not use CMC email addresses to register for personal services, social media, or non-work subscriptions.
  6.3 [BINDING]: Mass emails to external recipients must be approved by the Communications Department before sending.

Section 7: VIOLATIONS AND CONSEQUENCES
  7.1: Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.
  7.2 [BINDING]: Violations involving unauthorised access to restricted data will be reported to law enforcement.
  7.3: CMC reserves the right to monitor, access, and audit any activity on CMC-owned systems at any time without prior notice.
