## PURPOSE AND SCOPE
1.1 This policy governs acceptable use of all IT systems, devices, networks, and data owned or managed by the City Municipal Corporation (CMC).
1.2 This policy applies to all permanent employees, contractual staff, and third-party vendors with CMC system access.
1.3 Use of CMC IT resources implies acceptance of this policy.

## CORPORATE DEVICES
2.1 [must] Corporate devices (laptops, desktops, mobile phones issued by CMC) must be used primarily for official work purposes.
2.2 [permitted / provided] Personal use of corporate devices is permitted in moderation, provided it does not interfere with work duties or consume excessive bandwidth.
2.3 [must] Employees must not install software on corporate devices without written approval from the IT Department.
2.4 [must] Software approved for installation must be sourced from the CMC-approved software catalogue only.
2.5 [must] Corporate devices must not be used to access gambling, adult content, or any site classified as harmful by the IT Department.
2.6 [must] Corporate devices must have the CMC endpoint security agent installed and active at all times. Disabling or circumventing this agent is a disciplinary offence.

## PERSONAL DEVICES (BYOD)
3.1 [may] Personal devices may be used to access CMC email and the CMC employee self-service portal only.
3.2 [must] Personal devices must not be used to access, store, or transmit classified or sensitive CMC data.
3.3 [must] Personal devices must not be connected to the CMC internal network (wired or wireless). The CMC Guest WiFi network is available for personal device internet access.
3.4 [must] Employees using personal devices for CMC email must enable device-level PIN or biometric lock.
3.5 [must] If a personal device containing CMC email is lost or stolen, the employee must report it to the IT helpdesk within 4 hours so that a remote wipe of CMC data can be performed.

## PASSWORDS AND ACCESS CONTROL
4.1 [must] Employees must not share their CMC system passwords with any other person, including IT staff.
4.2 [will] IT staff will never ask for your password. Any request for your password should be reported to the IT Security team.
4.3 [must] Passwords must be changed every 90 days as prompted by the system.
4.4 [mandatory] Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all remote access to CMC systems.

## DATA HANDLING
5.1 [must] CMC data classified as Confidential or Restricted must not be stored on personal devices, personal cloud storage, or any system not approved by the IT Department.
5.2 [must] Employees must not forward CMC email containing Confidential data to personal email accounts.
5.3 [must] Printing of Confidential documents must use the secure print function. Documents must not be left unattended at printers.

## INTERNET AND EMAIL USE
6.1 Internet use on CMC systems is monitored and logged.
6.2 [must] Employees must not use CMC email addresses to register for personal services, social media, or non-work subscriptions.
6.3 [must] Mass emails to external recipients must be approved by the Communications Department before sending.

## VIOLATIONS AND CONSEQUENCES
7.1 [may] Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.
7.2 [will] Violations involving unauthorised access to restricted data will be reported to law enforcement.
7.3 [reserves the right] CMC reserves the right to monitor, access, and audit any activity on CMC-owned systems at any time without prior notice.
