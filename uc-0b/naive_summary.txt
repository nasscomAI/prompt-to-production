# City Municipal Corporation HR Leave Policy Summary

This policy outlines the leave entitlements for permanent and contractual employees at the City Municipal Corporation (CMC). As is standard practice in government organisations, daily wage workers and consultants are excluded.

## Annual Leave
Employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per year, accruing at 1.5 days per month. You must submit a leave application 14 days in advance and get approval from your manager. Unapproved absence will be treated as Loss of Pay (LOP). You can carry forward up to 5 days of unused leave to the next year, but employees are generally expected to use them by March.

## Sick Leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave annually. If you are sick for 3 or more days, a medical certificate is required. Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the following year.

## Maternity and Paternity Leave
Female employees receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for their first two children, and 12 weeks for any subsequent children. Male employees get 5 days of paternity leave, which must be taken within 30 days of birth.

## Leave Without Pay (LWP)
LWP can be requested once all paid leave is exhausted. This requires approval from the Department Head. Extended LWP over 30 days needs the Municipal Commissioner's approval.

## Public Holidays & Encashment
Employees get all standard gazetted public holidays. Working on a holiday grants a compensatory off day. Annual leave can only be encashed upon retirement or resignation (up to 60 days). Sick leave and LWP cannot be encashed.

## Grievances
Any leave-related grievances should be directed to the HR Department within 10 working days.
