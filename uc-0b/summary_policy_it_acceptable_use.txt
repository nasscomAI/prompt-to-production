[1.1] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] This policy governs acceptable use of all IT systems, devices, networks, and data owned or managed by the City Municipal Corporation (CMC).
[1.2] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] This policy applies to all permanent employees, contractual staff, and third-party vendors with CMC system access.
[1.3] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Use of CMC IT resources implies acceptance of this policy.
[2.1] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Corporate devices (laptops, desktops, mobile phones issued by CMC) must be used primarily for official work purposes.
[2.2] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Personal use of corporate devices is permitted in moderation, provided it does not interfere with work duties or consume excessive bandwidth.
[2.3] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Employees must not install software on corporate devices without written approval from the IT Department.
[2.4] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Software approved for installation must be sourced from the CMC-approved software catalogue only.
[2.5] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Corporate devices must not be used to access gambling, adult content, or any site classified as harmful by the IT Department.
[2.6] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Corporate devices must have the CMC endpoint security agent installed and active at all times. Disabling or circumventing this agent is a disciplinary offence.
[3.1] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Personal devices may be used to access CMC email and the CMC employee self-service portal only.
[3.2] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Personal devices must not be used to access, store, or transmit classified or sensitive CMC data.
[3.3] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Personal devices must not be connected to the CMC internal network (wired or wireless). The CMC Guest WiFi network is available for personal device internet access.
[3.4] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Employees using personal devices for CMC email must enable device-level PIN or biometric lock.
[3.5] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] If a personal device containing CMC email is lost or stolen, the employee must report it to the IT helpdesk within 4 hours so that a remote wipe of CMC data can be performed.
[4.1] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Employees must not share their CMC system passwords with any other person, including IT staff.
[4.2] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] IT staff will never ask for your password. Any request for your password should be reported to the IT Security team.
[4.3] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Passwords must be changed every 90 days as prompted by the system.
[4.4] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all remote access to CMC systems.
[5.1] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] CMC data classified as Confidential or Restricted must not be stored on personal devices, personal cloud storage, or any system not approved by the IT Department.
[5.2] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Employees must not forward CMC email containing Confidential data to personal email accounts.
[5.3] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Printing of Confidential documents must use the secure print function. Documents must not be left unattended at printers.
[6.1] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Internet use on CMC systems is monitored and logged.
[6.2] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Employees must not use CMC email addresses to register for personal services, social media, or non-work subscriptions.
[6.3] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Mass emails to external recipients must be approved by the Communications Department before sending.
[7.1] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.
[7.2] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] Violations involving unauthorised access to restricted data will be reported to law enforcement.
[7.3] [FLAGGED — quoted verbatim] CMC reserves the right to monitor, access, and audit any activity on CMC-owned systems at any time without prior notice.
