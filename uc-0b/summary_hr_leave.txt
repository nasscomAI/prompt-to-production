CMC EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY — CRITICAL CLAUSES SUMMARY
Source Document: HR-POL-001 (Version: 2.3)
==================================================

Clause 2.3 Summary: 14-day advance notice is required for annual leave using Form HR-L1.
  [Verbatim: "Employees must submit a leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1."]

Clause 2.4 Summary: Written approval from direct manager is required before leave commences. Verbal approval is not valid.
  [Verbatim: "Leave applications must receive written approval from the employee's direct manager before the leave commences. Verbal approval is not valid."]

Clause 2.5 Summary: Unapproved absence will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval.
  [Verbatim: "Unapproved absence will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval."]

Clause 2.6 Summary: Maximum of 5 unused annual leave days can be carried forward; any days exceeding 5 are forfeited on 31 December.
  [Verbatim: "Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year. Any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December."]

Clause 2.7 Summary: Carry-forward annual leave days must be used in Jan–Mar (first quarter) of the following year or they are forfeited.
  [Verbatim: "Carry-forward days must be used within the first quarter (January–March) of the following year or they are forfeited. ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════"]

Clause 3.2 Summary: Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 48 hours of returning to work.
  [Verbatim: "Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 48 hours of returning to work."]

Clause 3.4 Summary: Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave period requires a medical certificate regardless of duration.
  [Verbatim: "Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave period requires a medical certificate regardless of duration. ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════"]

Clause 5.2 Summary: Leave Without Pay (LWP) requires approval from both the Department Head and the HR Director. Manager approval alone is not sufficient.
  [Verbatim: "LWP requires approval from the Department Head and the HR Director. Manager approval alone is not sufficient."]

Clause 5.3 Summary: Leave Without Pay (LWP) exceeding 30 continuous days requires approval from the Municipal Commissioner.
  [Verbatim: "LWP exceeding 30 continuous days requires approval from the Municipal Commissioner."]

Clause 7.2 Summary: Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances.
  [Verbatim: "Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances."]
