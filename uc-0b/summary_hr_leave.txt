HR Leave Policy Summary

1. Employees must submit leave applications at least 14 days in advance.
2. Written approval is mandatory before leave begins. Verbal approval is not accepted.
3. Unapproved absence results in Loss of Pay even if approved later.
4. A maximum of 5 leave days may be carried forward. Extra days are forfeited on 31 December.
5. Carry-forward leave must be used between January and March or it is forfeited.
6. Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate within 48 hours.
7. Sick leave before or after holidays also requires a medical certificate regardless of duration.
8. Leave Without Pay requires approval from BOTH the Department Head AND the HR Director.
9. Leave Without Pay exceeding 30 days additionally requires Municipal Commissioner approval.
10. Leave encashment during service is not permitted.
