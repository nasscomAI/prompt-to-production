CITY MUNICIPAL CORPORATION — HR DEPARTMENT
SUMMARY OF EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY (HR-POL-001 v2.3)
Effective Date: 1 April 2024
================================================================================

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
- [Clause 1.1] Governs all leave entitlements for permanent and contractual employees of City Municipal Corporation (CMC).
- [Clause 1.2] Excludes daily wage workers and consultants (governed strictly by separate contracts).

2. ANNUAL LEAVE
- [Clause 2.1] Entitlement: 18 paid annual leave days per calendar year for permanent employees.
- [Clause 2.2] Accrual: Accrues at 1.5 days per month from the date of joining.
- [Clause 2.3] Notice Period: Employees MUST submit a leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1.
- [Clause 2.4] Mandatory Approval: Leave MUST receive written approval from the direct manager before leave commences. Verbal approval is NOT valid.
- [Clause 2.5] Unauthorized Absence: Unapproved absence WILL be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of any subsequent approval.
- [Clause 2.6] Carry-Forward Limits: Employees MAY carry forward a maximum of 5 unused days to the following year. Any days above 5 ARE FORFEITED on 31 December.
- [Clause 2.7] Carry-Forward Expiry: Carry-forward days MUST be used within Q1 (January–March) of the following year or they ARE FORFEITED.

3. SICK LEAVE
- [Clause 3.1] Entitlement: 12 paid sick leave days per calendar year.
- [Clause 3.2] Medical Certificate (Duration): Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days REQUIRES a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 48 hours of returning to work.
- [Clause 3.3] No Carry-Forward: Sick leave CANNOT be carried forward to the following year.
- [Clause 3.4] Medical Certificate (Adjacent to Holidays): Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave period REQUIRES a medical certificate regardless of duration.

4. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
- [Clause 4.1] Maternity (1st & 2nd births): 26 weeks paid leave for the first two live births.
- [Clause 4.2] Maternity (Subsequent): 12 weeks paid leave for a third or subsequent child.
- [Clause 4.3] Paternity: 5 days paid leave for male employees, to be taken within 30 days of child's birth.
- [Clause 4.4] Paternity Continuity: Paternity leave CANNOT be split across multiple periods.

5. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)
- [Clause 5.1] Prerequisite: LWP may only be applied for after exhausting ALL applicable paid leave entitlements.
- [Clause 5.2] Dual Approval Requirement: LWP REQUIRES approval from BOTH the Department Head AND the HR Director. Manager approval alone is NOT sufficient.
- [Clause 5.3] Extended LWP (>30 Days): LWP exceeding 30 continuous days REQUIRES approval from the Municipal Commissioner.
- [Clause 5.4] Impact on Service: Periods of LWP DO NOT count toward service for seniority, increments, or retirement benefits.

6. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS & COMPENSATORY OFF
- [Clause 6.1] Public Holidays: Entitled to all State Government gazetted public holidays.
- [Clause 6.2] Compensatory Off: Working on a public holiday entitles the employee to 1 compensatory off day, to be taken within 60 days of the holiday worked.
- [Clause 6.3] No Encashment: Compensatory off CANNOT be encashed.

7. LEAVE ENCASHMENT
- [Clause 7.1] Encashment at Separation: Annual leave may be encashed ONLY at retirement or resignation (maximum 60 days).
- [Clause 7.2] Strict Prohibition During Service: Leave encashment during service is NOT PERMITTED under any circumstances.
- [Clause 7.3] Sick & LWP: Sick leave and LWP CANNOT be encashed under any circumstances.

8. GRIEVANCES
- [Clause 8.1] Timeline: Leave-related grievances MUST be raised with HR Department within 10 working days of the disputed decision.
- [Clause 8.2] Late Submissions: Grievances after 10 working days will NOT be considered unless exceptional circumstances are demonstrated in writing.
