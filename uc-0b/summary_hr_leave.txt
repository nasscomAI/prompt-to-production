SECTION: 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Clause 1.1: Governs all leave entitlements for permanent and contractual employees of the City Municipal Corporation (CMC).

Clause 1.2: Does not apply to daily wage workers or consultants, who are strictly governed by their respective contracts.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION: 2. ANNUAL LEAVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Clause 2.1: Permanent employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Clause 2.2: Annual leave accrues at 1.5 days per month from the date of joining.

Clause 2.3: Employees must submit a leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1. (Binding verb: must)

Clause 2.4: Written approval from the employee's direct manager must be received before leave commences; verbal approval is not valid. (Binding verb: must)
  [FLAG: VERBATIM QUOTE - CRITICAL BINDING OBLIGATION]
  Source Text: "Leave applications must receive written approval from the employee's direct manager before the leave commences. Verbal approval is not valid."

Clause 2.5: Unapproved absence will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval. (Binding verb: will)
  [FLAG: VERBATIM QUOTE - CRITICAL BINDING OBLIGATION]
  Source Text: "Unapproved absence will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval."

Clause 2.6: Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following year; any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December. (Binding verbs: may / are forfeited)

Clause 2.7: Carry-forward days must be used within the first quarter (January–March) of the following year or they are forfeited. (Binding verb: must / are forfeited)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION: 3. SICK LEAVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Clause 3.1: Each employee is entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.

Clause 3.2: Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 48 hours of returning to work. (Binding verb: requires)

Clause 3.3: Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the following year.

Clause 3.4: Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave period requires a medical certificate regardless of duration. (Binding verb: requires)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION: 4. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Clause 4.1: Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two live births.

Clause 4.2: For a third or subsequent child, paid maternity leave is 12 weeks.

Clause 4.3: Male employees are entitled to 5 days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 30 days of the child's birth.

Clause 4.4: Paternity leave cannot be split across multiple periods.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION: 5. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Clause 5.1: An employee may apply for Leave Without Pay (LWP) only after exhausting all applicable paid leave entitlements.

Clause 5.2: LWP requires approval from BOTH the Department Head AND the HR Director; manager approval alone is not sufficient. (Binding verb: requires | Dual Approval)
  [FLAG: VERBATIM QUOTE - CRITICAL BINDING OBLIGATION]
  Source Text: "LWP requires approval from the Department Head and the HR Director. Manager approval alone is not sufficient."

Clause 5.3: LWP exceeding 30 continuous days requires approval from the Municipal Commissioner. (Binding verb: requires)

Clause 5.4: Periods of LWP do not count toward service for seniority, increments, or retirement benefits.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION: 6. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Clause 6.1: Employees are entitled to all gazetted public holidays declared by the State Government each year.

Clause 6.2: Employees required to work on a public holiday are entitled to one compensatory off day, to be taken within 60 days of the holiday worked.

Clause 6.3: Compensatory off cannot be encashed under any circumstances.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION: 7. LEAVE ENCASHMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Clause 7.1: Annual leave may be encashed only at the time of retirement or resignation, subject to a maximum of 60 days.

Clause 7.2: Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances. (Binding verb: not permitted)
  [FLAG: VERBATIM QUOTE - CRITICAL BINDING OBLIGATION]
  Source Text: "Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances."

Clause 7.3: Sick leave and LWP cannot be encashed under any circumstances.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION: 8. GRIEVANCES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Clause 8.1: Leave-related grievances must be raised with the HR Department within 10 working days of the disputed decision. (Binding verb: must)

Clause 8.2: Grievances raised after 10 working days will not be considered unless exceptional circumstances are demonstrated in writing.
