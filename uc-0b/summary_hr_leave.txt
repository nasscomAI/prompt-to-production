HR Leave Policy Summary

1. Permanent employees receive 18 days of annual leave each year.

2. Annual leave applications must be submitted at least 14 calendar
   days in advance using Form HR-L1.

3. Leave requires written approval before it begins.
   Verbal approval is not valid.

4. Unapproved absence will be treated as Loss of Pay (LOP)
   regardless of later approval.

5. A maximum of 5 unused leave days may be carried forward.
   Days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December.

6. Carry-forward leave must be used between January and March
   or it is forfeited.

7. Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical
   certificate within 48 hours of returning to work.

8. Sick leave immediately before or after a public holiday or
   annual leave also requires a medical certificate regardless
   of duration.

9. Leave Without Pay (LWP) requires approval from BOTH the
   Department Head AND the HR Director.

10. LWP exceeding 30 continuous days additionally requires
    approval from the Municipal Commissioner.

11. Leave encashment during service is NOT permitted under
    any circumstances.
