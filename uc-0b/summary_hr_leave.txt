SUMMARY OF EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY (HR-POL-001)
=============================================
This summary contains the binding obligations extracted from the employee leave policy.
All statements here represent strict conditions with no exceptions unless specified in the text.

[FLAGGED FOR VERBATIM ACCURACY] Clause 2.3:
Verbatim Text: "Employees must submit a leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1."
- Core Obligation: Submit leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1.
- Binding Verb: must

[FLAGGED FOR VERBATIM ACCURACY] Clause 2.4:
Verbatim Text: "Leave applications must receive written approval from the employee's direct manager before the leave commences. Verbal approval is not valid."
- Core Obligation: Written approval must be obtained from the direct manager before leave commences; verbal approval is invalid.
- Binding Verb: must

[FLAGGED FOR VERBATIM ACCURACY] Clause 2.5:
Verbatim Text: "Unapproved absence will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval."
- Core Obligation: Unapproved absence will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval.
- Binding Verb: will

[FLAGGED FOR VERBATIM ACCURACY] Clause 2.6:
Verbatim Text: "Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year. Any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December."
- Core Obligation: Maximum 5 days annual leave carry-forward; any days exceeding 5 are forfeited on 31 December.
- Binding Verb: may / are forfeited

[FLAGGED FOR VERBATIM ACCURACY] Clause 2.7:
Verbatim Text: "Carry-forward days must be used within the first quarter (January–March) of the following year or they are forfeited."
- Core Obligation: Carry-forward days must be used between January and March (first quarter) or they are forfeited.
- Binding Verb: must / are forfeited

[FLAGGED FOR VERBATIM ACCURACY] Clause 3.2:
Verbatim Text: "Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 48 hours of returning to work."
- Core Obligation: Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate submitted within 48 hours of return.
- Binding Verb: requires

[FLAGGED FOR VERBATIM ACCURACY] Clause 3.4:
Verbatim Text: "Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave period requires a medical certificate regardless of duration."
- Core Obligation: Sick leave taken immediately before or after a holiday or annual leave requires a certificate regardless of duration.
- Binding Verb: requires

[FLAGGED FOR VERBATIM ACCURACY] Clause 5.2:
Verbatim Text: "LWP requires approval from the Department Head and the HR Director. Manager approval alone is not sufficient."
- Core Obligation: Leave Without Pay (LWP) requires approval from both the Department Head and the HR Director; manager approval alone is insufficient.
- Binding Verb: requires

[FLAGGED FOR VERBATIM ACCURACY] Clause 5.3:
Verbatim Text: "LWP exceeding 30 continuous days requires approval from the Municipal Commissioner."
- Core Obligation: LWP exceeding 30 continuous days requires approval from the Municipal Commissioner.
- Binding Verb: requires

[FLAGGED FOR VERBATIM ACCURACY] Clause 7.2:
Verbatim Text: "Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances."
- Core Obligation: Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances.
- Binding Verb: not permitted
