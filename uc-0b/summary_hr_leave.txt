CITY MUNICIPAL CORPORATION — HR LEAVE POLICY SUMMARY (HR-POL-001 v2.3)

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
- Governs leave entitlements for permanent (18 days/yr) and contractual employees. Does not apply to daily wage workers or consultants (Sections 1.1, 1.2).

2. ANNUAL LEAVE
- Section 2.1 & 2.2: Permanent employees get 18 paid days per year, accruing at 1.5 days per month.
- Clause 2.3 [CRITICAL]: Employees MUST submit leave applications at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1.
- Clause 2.4 [CRITICAL]: MUST receive written approval from direct manager BEFORE leave commences. Verbal approval is NOT valid.
- Clause 2.5 [CRITICAL]: Unapproved absence WILL be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval.
- Clause 2.6 [CRITICAL]: Employees MAY carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days; days above 5 ARE FORFEITED on 31 December.
- Clause 2.7 [CRITICAL]: Carry-forward days MUST be used within Q1 (January–March) of following year or they ARE FORFEITED.

3. SICK LEAVE
- Section 3.1 & 3.3: Entitled to 12 paid sick days per year; cannot be carried forward.
- Clause 3.2 [CRITICAL]: Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days REQUIRES a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 48 hours of returning to work.
- Clause 3.4 [CRITICAL]: Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave period REQUIRES a medical certificate regardless of duration.

4. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
- Section 4.1 & 4.2: 26 weeks paid maternity leave for first 2 births; 12 weeks for third or subsequent child.
- Section 4.3 & 4.4: Male employees get 5 paid paternity days (within 30 days of birth, cannot be split).

5. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)
- Section 5.1 & 5.4: Apply only after exhausting paid leave. LWP does not count toward service seniority, increments, or retirement benefits.
- Clause 5.2 [CRITICAL]: LWP REQUIRES approval from BOTH the Department Head AND the HR Director. Manager approval alone is NOT sufficient.
- Clause 5.3 [CRITICAL]: LWP exceeding 30 continuous days REQUIRES approval from the Municipal Commissioner.

6. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
- Section 6.1-6.3: Gazetted public holidays apply. Work on holiday yields 1 compensatory off day (must be taken within 60 days, cannot be encashed).

7. LEAVE ENCASHMENT
- Section 7.1 & 7.3: Annual leave encashment allowed ONLY at retirement/resignation (max 60 days). Sick leave & LWP cannot be encashed.
- Clause 7.2 [CRITICAL]: Leave encashment during service is NOT PERMITTED under any circumstances.

8. GRIEVANCES
- Section 8.1 & 8.2: Grievances must be raised with HR within 10 working days of disputed decision.
