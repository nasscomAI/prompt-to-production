### Employee Leave Policy Summary (HR-POL-001)

This summary outlines the mandatory leave regulations for the City Municipal Corporation.

**Annual Leave & Carry-Forward**
*   **Advance Notice:** Employees must submit leave applications at least 14 calendar days in advance (Clause 2.3).
*   **Approval Process:** Written approval from the direct manager is mandatory before leave commences; verbal approval is not valid (Clause 2.4).
*   **Unapproved Absence:** Any absence taken without prior approval will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP), even if approval is granted at a later date (Clause 2.5).
*   **Carry-Forward Limits:** A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried forward; any balance exceeding 5 days is forfeited on 31 December (Clause 2.6).
*   **Usage Window:** Carried-forward days must be utilized between January and March of the following year, or they will be forfeited (Clause 2.7).

**Sick Leave**
*   **Medical Certification:** For sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days, a medical certificate from a registered practitioner must be submitted within 48 hours of returning to work (Clause 3.2).
*   **Holiday/Leave Proximity:** If sick leave is taken immediately before or after a public holiday or an annual leave period, a medical certificate is required regardless of the duration of the leave (Clause 3.4).

**Leave Without Pay (LWP)**
*   **Approval Requirements:** LWP requires formal approval from both the Department Head and the HR Director (Clause 5.2).
*   **Extended Leave:** Any LWP exceeding 30 continuous days requires additional approval from the Municipal Commissioner (Clause 5.3).

**Leave Encashment**
*   **Prohibition:** "Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances." [VERBATIM] (Clause 7.2).
