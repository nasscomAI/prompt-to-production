UC-0B — HR Leave Policy Summary
Source: policy_hr_leave.txt
============================================================

[1.1]
Summary: This policy governs all leave entitlements for permanent and contractual employees of the City Municipal Corporation (CMC).

[1.2]
Summary: This policy does not apply to daily wage workers or consultants. Those categories are governed by their respective contracts.

[2.1]
Summary: Each permanent employee is entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

[2.2]
Summary: Annual leave accrues at 1.5 days per month from the date of joining.

[2.3]
Summary: Employees must submit a leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1.

[2.4]
Summary: Leave applications must receive written approval from the employee's direct manager before the leave commences. Verbal approval is not valid.
Flag: VERBATIM_REQUIRED

[2.5]
Summary: Unapproved absence will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval.
Flag: VERBATIM_REQUIRED

[2.6]
Summary: Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year. Any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December.

[2.7]
Summary: Carry-forward days must be used within the first quarter (January–March) of the following year or they are forfeited.

[3.1]
Summary: Each employee is entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.

[3.2]
Summary: Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 48 hours of returning to work.

[3.3]
Summary: Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the following year.

[3.4]
Summary: Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave period requires a medical certificate regardless of duration.
Flag: VERBATIM_REQUIRED

[4.1]
Summary: Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two live births.

[4.2]
Summary: For a third or subsequent child, maternity leave is 12 weeks paid.

[4.3]
Summary: Male employees are entitled to 5 days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 30 days of the child's birth.

[4.4]
Summary: Paternity leave cannot be split across multiple periods.
Flag: VERBATIM_REQUIRED

[5.1]
Summary: An employee may apply for Leave Without Pay only after exhausting all applicable paid leave entitlements.

[5.2]
Summary: LWP requires approval from the Department Head and the HR Director. Manager approval alone is not sufficient.
Flag: VERBATIM_REQUIRED

[5.3]
Summary: LWP exceeding 30 continuous days requires approval from the Municipal Commissioner.

[5.4]
Summary: Periods of LWP do not count toward service for the purposes of seniority, increments, or retirement benefits.

[6.1]
Summary: Employees are entitled to all gazetted public holidays as declared by the State Government each year.

[6.2]
Summary: If an employee is required to work on a public holiday, they are entitled to one compensatory off day, to be taken within 60 days of the holiday worked.

[6.3]
Summary: Compensatory off cannot be encashed.
Flag: VERBATIM_REQUIRED

[7.1]
Summary: Annual leave may be encashed only at the time of retirement or resignation, subject to a maximum of 60 days.

[7.2]
Summary: Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances.
Flag: VERBATIM_REQUIRED

[7.3]
Summary: Sick leave and LWP cannot be encashed under any circumstances.
Flag: VERBATIM_REQUIRED

[8.1]
Summary: Leave-related grievances must be raised with the HR Department within 10 working days of the disputed decision.

[8.2]
Summary: Grievances raised after 10 working days will not be considered unless exceptional circumstances are demonstrated in writing.
Flag: VERBATIM_REQUIRED
