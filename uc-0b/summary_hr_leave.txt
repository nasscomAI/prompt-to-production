SECTION 2 — ANNUAL LEAVE
======================================================================
[2.1]  binding verb: IS ENTITLED
      obligation : Each permanent employee is entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

[2.2]  binding verb: STATEMENT
      obligation : Annual leave accrues at 1.5 days per month from the date of joining.

[2.3]  binding verb: MUST
      obligation : Employees must submit a leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1.
      qualifiers : at least

[2.4]  binding verb: MUST
      obligation : Leave applications must receive written approval from the employee's direct manager before the leave commences.
      condition  : Verbal approval is not valid.
      qualifiers : before, not valid

[2.5]  binding verb: WILL
      obligation : Unapproved absence will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval.
      qualifiers : regardless

[2.6]  binding verb: MAY
      obligation : Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year.
      condition  : Any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December.
      qualifiers : maximum

[2.7]  binding verb: MUST
      obligation : Carry-forward days must be used within the first quarter (January–March) of the following year or they are forfeited.
      qualifiers : within

SECTION 3 — SICK LEAVE
======================================================================
[3.1]  binding verb: IS ENTITLED
      obligation : Each employee is entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.

[3.2]  binding verb: REQUIRES

      qualifiers : within

[3.3]  binding verb: CANNOT

      qualifiers : after, before, regardless

SECTION 4 — MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
======================================================================
[4.1]  binding verb: ARE ENTITLED
      obligation : Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two live births.

[4.2]  binding verb: STATEMENT
      obligation : For a third or subsequent child, maternity leave is 12 weeks paid.

[4.3]  binding verb: ARE ENTITLED
