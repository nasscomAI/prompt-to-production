CITY MUNICIPAL CORPORATION — HR LEAVE POLICY SUMMARY (HR-POL-001 v2.3)

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
- Clause 1.1: Applies to all permanent and contractual employees of City Municipal Corporation (CMC).
- Clause 1.2: Does not apply to daily wage workers or consultants (governed by separate contracts).

2. ANNUAL LEAVE
- Clause 2.1: Permanent employees receive 18 days paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Clause 2.2: Accrual rate is 1.5 days per month from joining date.
- Clause 2.3: Employees MUST submit leave applications at least 14 calendar days in advance via Form HR-L1.
- Clause 2.4: Applications MUST receive written approval from the direct manager prior to leave commencement. Verbal approval is NOT valid.
- Clause 2.5: Unapproved absence WILL be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval.
- Clause 2.6: Maximum 5 days carry-forward allowed to following year. Days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December.
- Clause 2.7: Carry-forward days MUST be used within Q1 (January–March) or are forfeited.

3. SICK LEAVE
- Clause 3.1: Entitlement of 12 days paid sick leave per calendar year.
- Clause 3.2: Sick leave of 3+ consecutive days REQUIRES a medical certificate from a registered practitioner submitted within 48 hours of return.
- Clause 3.3: Sick leave cannot be carried forward.
- Clause 3.4: Sick leave immediately before or after a public holiday/annual leave REQUIRES a medical certificate regardless of duration.

4. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
- Clause 4.1: Female employees receive 26 weeks paid maternity leave for the first 2 live births.
- Clause 4.2: 12 weeks paid maternity leave for third or subsequent children.
- Clause 4.3: Male employees receive 5 days paid paternity leave within 30 days of birth.
- Clause 4.4: Paternity leave cannot be split across multiple periods.

5. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)
- Clause 5.1: LWP available only after exhausting all paid leave entitlements.
- Clause 5.2: LWP REQUIRES dual approval from BOTH the Department Head AND the HR Director (Manager approval alone is NOT sufficient).
- Clause 5.3: LWP exceeding 30 continuous days REQUIRES approval from the Municipal Commissioner.
- Clause 5.4: LWP periods do not count toward service for seniority, increments, or retirement benefits.

6. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
- Clause 6.1: Entitled to state gazetted public holidays.
- Clause 6.2: Working on a public holiday grants 1 compensatory off day to be used within 60 days.
- Clause 6.3: Compensatory off cannot be encashed.

7. LEAVE ENCASHMENT
- Clause 7.1: Annual leave encashment permitted only upon retirement or resignation (max 60 days).
- Clause 7.2: Leave encashment during active service is NOT permitted under any circumstances.
- Clause 7.3: Sick leave and LWP cannot be encashed under any circumstances.

8. GRIEVANCES
- Clause 8.1: Leave grievances MUST be raised with HR within 10 working days of decision.
- Clause 8.2: Grievances after 10 working days will not be considered without written proof of exceptional circumstances.
