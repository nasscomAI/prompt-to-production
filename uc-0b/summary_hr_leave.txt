Here is a summary of the City Municipal Corporation’s Employee Leave Policy (HR-POL-001):

### **1. Scope**
* Applies to permanent and contractual employees only. Daily wage workers and consultants are excluded.

### **2. Annual Leave**
* **Entitlement:** 18 days per year, accruing at 1.5 days per month.
* **Application Process:** Must apply 14 days in advance using Form HR-L1, with written approval from a direct manager. Verbal approval is invalid, and unapproved absences result in Loss of Pay (LOP).
* **Carry-Forward:** A maximum of 5 unused days can be carried into the next year, but they must be used between January and March, or they are forfeited.

### **3. Sick Leave**
* **Entitlement:** 12 days per year (cannot be carried forward).
* **Documentation:** A medical certificate is required for 3+ consecutive days (submitted within 48 hours of return) or if sick leave is taken immediately before/after a public holiday or annual leave.

### **4. Maternity & Paternity Leave**
* **Maternity:** 26 weeks paid for the first two live births; 12 weeks paid for the third and subsequent children.
* **Paternity:** 5 days paid, taken all at once within 30 days of the child's birth.

### **5. Leave Without Pay (LWP)**
* **Conditions:** Only available after all paid leave is exhausted.
* **Approvals:** Requires Department Head and HR Director approval (plus the Municipal Commissioner for over 30 continuous days).
* **Impact:** LWP does not count toward seniority, salary increments, or retirement benefits.

### **6. Public Holidays & Compensatory Offs**
* Employees receive all State-declared gazetted holidays.
* Working on a public holiday grants a compensatory off day (non-encashable) to be used within 60 days.

### **7. Leave Encashment**
* Annual leave can only be encashed upon retirement or resignation (up to a maximum of 60 days); encashment during service is prohibited. 
* Sick leave and LWP cannot be encashed.

### **8. Grievances**
* Leave disputes must be submitted to the HR Department in writing within 10 working days.
