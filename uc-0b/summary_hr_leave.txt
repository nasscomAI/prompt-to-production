HR Leave Policy Summary

1. Clause 2.3: A 14-day advance notice is required for taking leave.
2. Clause 2.4: Written approval is required before leave commences. Verbal approval is not valid.
3. Clause 2.5: Unapproved absence will result in Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval.
4. Clause 2.6: A maximum of 5 days can be carried forward. Any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 Dec.
5. Clause 2.7: Carry-forward days must be used between Jan–Mar or they will be forfeited.
6. Clause 3.2: Taking 3+ consecutive sick days requires a medical certificate within 48 hours.
7. Clause 3.4: Sick leave immediately before or after a holiday requires a medical certificate regardless of duration.
8. Clause 5.2: Leave Without Pay (LWP) requires approval from BOTH the Department Head AND the HR Director.
9. Clause 5.3: Leave Without Pay (LWP) >30 days requires Municipal Commissioner approval.
10. Clause 7.2: Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances.
