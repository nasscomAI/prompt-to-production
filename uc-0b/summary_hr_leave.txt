=== HR POLICY SUMMARY ===

1. OVERVIEW & SCOPE:
This document outlines binding leave policies, eligibility criteria, and notice requirements.

2. KEY BINDING OBLIGATIONS & CONDITIONS:
- Casual Leave: Requires prior approval from line manager; subject to team coverage constraints.
- Earned Leave: Must be applied at least 14 days in advance.
- Sick Leave: Medical certificate mandatory for leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.
- Unexcused Absences: May result in disciplinary action as per company standing orders.

3. ENFORCEMENT & COMPLIANCE:
All employee leave applications must strictly follow formal submission channels.
