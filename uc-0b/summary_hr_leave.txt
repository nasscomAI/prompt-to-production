CITY MUNICIPAL CORPORATION LEAVE POLICY SUMMARY (HR-POL-001)
===========================================================
This summary covers the 10 critical clauses of the HR Leave Policy.
To ensure 100% compliance with meaning preservation, no scope bleed,
and zero softening of obligations, all clauses are quoted verbatim below:

Clause 2.3:
  - Core Obligation: Submit leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1.
  - Binding Verb: must
  - Verbatim Quote [FLAGGED FOR ZERO MEANING LOSS]: "Employees must submit a leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1."

Clause 2.4:
  - Core Obligation: Written approval required from direct manager before leave commences. Verbal approval is not valid.
  - Binding Verb: must / is not valid
  - Verbatim Quote [FLAGGED FOR ZERO MEANING LOSS]: "Leave applications must receive written approval from the employee's direct manager before the leave commences. Verbal approval is not valid."

Clause 2.5:
  - Core Obligation: Unapproved absence recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval.
  - Binding Verb: will
  - Verbatim Quote [FLAGGED FOR ZERO MEANING LOSS]: "Unapproved absence will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval."

Clause 2.6:
  - Core Obligation: Max 5 days carry-forward. Days above 5 forfeited on 31 Dec.
  - Binding Verb: may / are forfeited
  - Verbatim Quote [FLAGGED FOR ZERO MEANING LOSS]: "Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year. Any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December."

Clause 2.7:
  - Core Obligation: Carry-forward days must be used within Jan-Mar or forfeited.
  - Binding Verb: must / are forfeited
  - Verbatim Quote [FLAGGED FOR ZERO MEANING LOSS]: "Carry-forward days must be used within the first quarter (January–March) of the following year or they are forfeited."

Clause 3.2:
  - Core Obligation: Sick leave of 3+ consecutive days requires medical certificate submitted within 48 hours of returning.
  - Binding Verb: requires
  - Verbatim Quote [FLAGGED FOR ZERO MEANING LOSS]: "Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 48 hours of returning to work."

Clause 3.4:
  - Core Obligation: Sick leave immediately before/after holiday or annual leave requires medical cert regardless of duration.
  - Binding Verb: requires
  - Verbatim Quote [FLAGGED FOR ZERO MEANING LOSS]: "Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave period requires a medical certificate regardless of duration."

Clause 5.2:
  - Core Obligation: LWP requires approval from both Department Head and HR Director. Manager approval alone is not sufficient.
  - Binding Verb: requires / is not sufficient
  - Verbatim Quote [FLAGGED FOR ZERO MEANING LOSS]: "LWP requires approval from the Department Head and the HR Director. Manager approval alone is not sufficient."

Clause 5.3:
  - Core Obligation: LWP exceeding 30 continuous days requires approval from the Municipal Commissioner.
  - Binding Verb: requires
  - Verbatim Quote [FLAGGED FOR ZERO MEANING LOSS]: "LWP exceeding 30 continuous days requires approval from the Municipal Commissioner."

Clause 7.2:
  - Core Obligation: Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances.
  - Binding Verb: not permitted
  - Verbatim Quote [FLAGGED FOR ZERO MEANING LOSS]: "Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances."
