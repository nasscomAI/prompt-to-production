=== POLICY SUMMARY AUDIT REPORT ===
Role: Legal and Policy Summarization Auditor

Summary of Clause 2.3:
  2.3 Employees must submit a leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1.

[VERBATIM RECOURSE FLAG - CLAUSE 2.4]
Clause 2.4 Leave applications must receive written approval from the employee's direct manager before the leave commences. Verbal approval is not valid.

Summary of Clause 2.5:
  2.5 Unapproved absence will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval.

Summary of Clause 2.6:
  2.6 Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year. Any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December.
