CITY MUNICIPAL CORPORATION — HR LEAVE POLICY SUMMARY
Document Reference: HR-POL-001 (Version 2.3)

MANDATORY CLAUSE INVENTORY & OBLIGATIONS:

[Clause 2.3] Annual Leave Application Notice:
Employees MUST submit a leave application using Form HR-L1 at least 14 calendar days in advance.

[Clause 2.4] Approval Requirement:
Leave applications MUST receive written approval from the employee's direct manager before leave commences. Verbal approval is NOT valid under any circumstances.

[Clause 2.5] Unapproved Absence:
Unapproved absence WILL be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP), regardless of any subsequent approval attempts.

[Clause 2.6] Carry-Forward Limits:
Employees MAY carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year. Any annual leave days above 5 ARE FORFEITED on 31 December.

[Clause 2.7] Carry-Forward Expiry:
Carry-forward leave days MUST be used within the first quarter (January–March) of the following year or they ARE FORFEITED.

[Clause 3.2] Sick Leave Medical Certificate:
Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days REQUIRES a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 48 hours of returning to work.

[Clause 3.4] Sick Leave Adjacent to Holidays/Leave:
Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave period REQUIRES a medical certificate regardless of duration.

[Clause 5.2] Leave Without Pay (LWP) Approvals:
LWP REQUIRES written approval from BOTH the Department Head AND the HR Director. Approval from the manager alone is NOT sufficient.

[Clause 5.3] Long-Term LWP Approval:
LWP exceeding 30 continuous days REQUIRES written approval from the Municipal Commissioner.

[Clause 7.2] In-Service Leave Encashment Prohibition:
Leave encashment during active service is NOT PERMITTED under any circumstances. (Annual leave encashment is permitted only upon retirement or resignation up to max 60 days per Clause 7.1).
