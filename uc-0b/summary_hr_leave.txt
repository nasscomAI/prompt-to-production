1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
====================================================================
[1.1] SCOPE
  This policy governs all leave entitlements for permanent and contractual employees of the City Municipal Corporation (CMC).

[1.2] SCOPE
  This policy does not apply to daily wage workers or consultants.
  Those categories are governed by their respective contracts.
  conditions: does not apply (NEGATION)

====================================================================
2. ANNUAL LEAVE
====================================================================
[2.1] ENTITLEMENT
  Each permanent employee is entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
  conditions: 18 days

[2.2] ENTITLEMENT
  Annual leave accrues at 1.5 days per month from the date of joining.
  conditions: 1.5 days

[2.3] MUST  [VERBATIM — multi-condition]
  Employees must submit a leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1.
  conditions: 14 calendar days · Form HR-L1

[2.4] MUST  [VERBATIM — multi-condition]
  Leave applications must receive written approval from the employee's direct manager before the leave commences.
  Verbal approval is not valid.
  conditions: direct manager · written approval · before the leave commences · is not valid (NEGATION)

[2.5] WILL  [VERBATIM — multi-condition]
  Unapproved absence will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval.
  conditions: Loss of Pay (LOP) · regardless of subsequent approval

[2.6] WILL  [VERBATIM — multi-condition]
  Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year.
  Any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December.
  conditions: 5 unused annual leave days · 31 December

[2.7] MUST  [VERBATIM — multi-condition]
  Carry-forward days must be used within the first quarter (January–March) of the following year or they are forfeited.
  conditions: January–March · first quarter

====================================================================
3. SICK LEAVE
====================================================================
[3.1] ENTITLEMENT
  Each employee is entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
  conditions: 12 days

[3.2] REQUIRES  [VERBATIM — multi-condition]
  Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 48 hours of returning to work.
  conditions: 3 or more consecutive days · 48 hours · registered medical practitioner · medical certificate

[3.3] PROHIBITED
  Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the following year.
  conditions: cannot (NEGATION)

[3.4] REQUIRES  [VERBATIM — multi-condition]
  Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave period requires a medical certificate regardless of duration.
  conditions: medical certificate · public holiday · regardless of duration

====================================================================
4. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
====================================================================
[4.1] ENTITLEMENT  [VERBATIM — multi-condition]
  Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two live births.
  conditions: 26 weeks · first two live births

[4.2] CLAUSE  [VERBATIM — multi-condition]
  For a third or subsequent child, maternity leave is 12 weeks paid.
  conditions: 12 weeks · third or subsequent child

[4.3] ENTITLEMENT  [VERBATIM — multi-condition]
  Male employees are entitled to 5 days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 30 days of the child's birth.
  conditions: 5 days · 30 days

[4.4] PROHIBITED
  Paternity leave cannot be split across multiple periods.
  conditions: cannot (NEGATION)

====================================================================
5. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)
====================================================================
[5.1] MAY
  An employee may apply for Leave Without Pay only after exhausting all applicable paid leave entitlements.
  conditions: only after exhausting

[5.2] REQUIRES  [VERBATIM — multi-condition]
  LWP requires approval from the Department Head and the HR Director.
  Manager approval alone is not sufficient.
  conditions: Department Head · HR Director · not sufficient (NEGATION)

[5.3] REQUIRES  [VERBATIM — multi-condition]
  LWP exceeding 30 continuous days requires approval from the Municipal Commissioner.
  conditions: 30 continuous days · Municipal Commissioner

[5.4] CLAUSE
  Periods of LWP do not count toward service for the purposes of seniority, increments, or retirement benefits.
  conditions: do not count (NEGATION)

====================================================================
6. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
====================================================================
[6.1] ENTITLEMENT
  Employees are entitled to all gazetted public holidays as declared by the State Government each year.
  conditions: State Government

[6.2] REQUIRES  [VERBATIM — multi-condition]
  If an employee is required to work on a public holiday, they are entitled to one compensatory off day, to be taken within 60 days of the holiday worked.
  conditions: 60 days · compensatory off · public holiday

[6.3] PROHIBITED  [VERBATIM — multi-condition]
  Compensatory off cannot be encashed.
  conditions: Compensatory off · cannot (NEGATION)

====================================================================
7. LEAVE ENCASHMENT
====================================================================
[7.1] MAY
  Annual leave may be encashed only at the time of retirement or resignation, subject to a maximum of 60 days.
  conditions: 60 days

[7.2] PROHIBITED  [VERBATIM — multi-condition]
  Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances.
  conditions: under any circumstances · not permitted (NEGATION)

[7.3] PROHIBITED  [VERBATIM — multi-condition]
  Sick leave and LWP cannot be encashed under any circumstances.
  conditions: under any circumstances · cannot (NEGATION)

====================================================================
8. GRIEVANCES
====================================================================
[8.1] MUST  [VERBATIM — multi-condition]
  Leave-related grievances must be raised with the HR Department within 10 working days of the disputed decision.
  conditions: 10 working days · HR Department

[8.2] WILL  [VERBATIM — multi-condition]
  Grievances raised after 10 working days will not be considered unless exceptional circumstances are demonstrated in writing.
  conditions: 10 working days · will not be considered (NEGATION)
