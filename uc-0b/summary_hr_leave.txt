EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY (HR-POL-001, v2.3) — CONDENSED COMPLIANT SUMMARY

1.1  Covers all leave entitlements of CMC permanent and contractual employees.
1.2  Excludes daily wage workers and consultants, governed by their own contracts.
2.1  Permanent employees are entitled to 18 days' paid annual leave per year.
2.2  Annual leave accrues 1.5 days/month from joining.
2.3  Employees must apply at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1.
2.4  Employees must receive written approval from the direct manager before leave; verbal approval is not valid.
2.5  Unapproved absence will be Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval.
2.6  Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 days; above 5 forfeited on 31 December.
2.7  Carried-over days must be used in January–March or forfeited.
3.1  Each employee is entitled to 12 days' paid sick leave per year.
3.2  Sick leave of 3+ consecutive days requires a medical certificate within 48 hours of return.
3.3  Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the following year. [QUOTED]
3.4  Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave period requires a medical certificate regardless of duration. [QUOTED]
4.1  Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks' paid maternity leave for first two live births.
4.2  Third or subsequent child: 12 weeks' paid maternity leave.
4.3  Male employees are entitled to 5 days' paid paternity leave within 30 days of the child's birth.
4.4  Paternity leave cannot be split across multiple periods. [QUOTED]
5.1  Employees may apply for LWP only after exhausting all applicable paid leave.
5.2  LWP requires approval from the Department Head and the HR Director; manager approval alone is not sufficient.
5.3  LWP exceeding 30 continuous days requires approval from the Municipal Commissioner. [QUOTED]
5.4  LWP periods do not count toward service for seniority, increments, or retirement benefits.
6.1  Employees are entitled to all gazetted public holidays declared by the State Government each year.
6.2  If an employee works a public holiday, they are entitled to one compensatory off day within 60 days.
6.3  Compensatory off cannot be encashed. [QUOTED]
7.1  Annual leave may be encashed only at retirement/resignation, max 60 days.
7.2  Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances. [QUOTED]
7.3  Sick leave and LWP cannot be encashed under any circumstances. [QUOTED]
8.1  Leave grievances must be raised with the HR Department within 10 working days of the disputed decision.
8.2  Grievances after 10 working days will not be considered unless exceptional circumstances are demonstrated in writing.
