Clause 1.1: Governs leave entitlements for permanent and contractual employees of the City Municipal Corporation (CMC).

Clause 1.2: Excludes daily wage workers and consultants, who are governed by respective contracts.

Clause 2.1: Permanent employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Clause 2.2: Annual leave accrues at 1.5 days per month from joining date.

Clause 2.3 [FLAG: VERBATIM] - Quoted verbatim to prevent meaning loss:
  "Employees must submit a leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1."

Clause 2.4 [FLAG: VERBATIM] - Quoted verbatim to prevent meaning loss:
  "Leave applications must receive written approval from the employee's direct manager before the leave commences. Verbal approval is not valid."

Clause 2.5 [FLAG: VERBATIM] - Quoted verbatim to prevent meaning loss:
  "Unapproved absence will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval."

Clause 2.6 [FLAG: VERBATIM] - Quoted verbatim to prevent meaning loss:
  "Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year. Any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December."

Clause 2.7 [FLAG: VERBATIM] - Quoted verbatim to prevent meaning loss:
  "Carry-forward days must be used within the first quarter (January–March) of the following year or they are forfeited."

Clause 3.1: Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.

Clause 3.2 [FLAG: VERBATIM] - Quoted verbatim to prevent meaning loss:
  "Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 48 hours of returning to work."

Clause 3.3: Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the following year.

Clause 3.4 [FLAG: VERBATIM] - Quoted verbatim to prevent meaning loss:
  "Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave period requires a medical certificate regardless of duration."

Clause 4.1: Female employees receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two live births.

Clause 4.2: Maternity leave is 12 weeks paid for the third or subsequent child.

Clause 4.3: Male employees receive 5 days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 30 days of the child's birth.

Clause 4.4: Paternity leave cannot be split across multiple periods.

Clause 5.1: LWP can be applied for only after exhausting all paid leave entitlements.

Clause 5.2 [FLAG: VERBATIM] - Quoted verbatim to prevent meaning loss:
  "LWP requires approval from the Department Head and the HR Director. Manager approval alone is not sufficient."

Clause 5.3 [FLAG: VERBATIM] - Quoted verbatim to prevent meaning loss:
  "LWP exceeding 30 continuous days requires approval from the Municipal Commissioner."

Clause 5.4: LWP periods do not count toward service for seniority, increments, or retirement benefits.

Clause 6.1: Employees are entitled to all State Government gazetted public holidays.

Clause 6.2: Working on a public holiday entitles the employee to one compensatory off day, to be taken within 60 days.

Clause 6.3: Compensatory off cannot be encashed.

Clause 7.1: Annual leave encashment is allowed only at retirement or resignation, capped at 60 days.

Clause 7.2 [FLAG: VERBATIM] - Quoted verbatim to prevent meaning loss:
  "Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances."

Clause 7.3: Sick leave and LWP cannot be encashed under any circumstances.

Clause 8.1: Grievances must be raised with HR within 10 working days of the disputed decision.

Clause 8.2: Grievances raised after 10 working days will not be considered unless exceptional circumstances are demonstrated in writing.
