ANNUAL LEAVE
----------------------------------------
2.3  Employees must submit a leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1.
2.4  Leave applications must receive written approval from the employee's direct manager before the leave commences. Verbal approval is not valid.
2.5  Unapproved absence will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval.
2.6 [MULTI_CONDITION] Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year. Any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December.
2.7 [MULTI_CONDITION] Carry-forward days must be used within the first quarter (January–March) of the following year or they are forfeited.

SICK LEAVE
----------------------------------------
3.2  Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 48 hours of returning to work.
3.4 [MULTI_CONDITION] Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave period requires a medical certificate regardless of duration.

LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)
----------------------------------------
5.2 [MULTI_CONDITION] LWP requires approval from the Department Head and the HR Director. Manager approval alone is not sufficient.
5.3 [MULTI_CONDITION] LWP exceeding 30 continuous days requires approval from the Municipal Commissioner.

LEAVE ENCASHMENT
----------------------------------------
7.2  Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances.
