CITY MUNICIPAL CORPORATION — HR LEAVE POLICY SUMMARY
Document Reference: HR-POL-001 | Version: 2.3

SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE
• Clause 1.1 & 1.2: Governs leave entitlements for permanent and contractual CMC employees. Daily wage workers and consultants are excluded.

SECTION 2: ANNUAL LEAVE (CLAUSES 2.1 – 2.7)
• Clause 2.1 & 2.2: Permanent employees earn 18 paid annual leave days per year, accruing at 1.5 days per month.
• Clause 2.3: Employees MUST submit leave applications at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1.
• Clause 2.4: Written approval MUST be obtained from the direct manager prior to leave commencement. Verbal approval is NOT valid.
• Clause 2.5: Unapproved absence WILL be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval.
• Clause 2.6: A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days MAY be carried forward; any excess above 5 days IS FORFEITED on 31 December.
• Clause 2.7: Carry-forward leave MUST be used within Q1 (January–March) or IS FORFEITED.

SECTION 3: SICK LEAVE (CLAUSES 3.1 – 3.4)
• Clause 3.1 & 3.3: 12 days paid sick leave per year. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.
• Clause 3.2: Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days REQUIRES a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner submitted within 48 hours of returning to work.
• Clause 3.4: Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave REQUIRES a medical certificate regardless of duration.

SECTION 4: MATERNITY & PATERNITY LEAVE (CLAUSES 4.1 – 4.4)
• Clause 4.1 & 4.2: Female employees receive 26 weeks paid maternity leave for the first two live births, and 12 weeks for subsequent births.
• Clause 4.3 & 4.4: Male employees receive 5 days paid paternity leave within 30 days of birth, which cannot be split across multiple periods.

SECTION 5: LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP) (CLAUSES 5.1 – 5.4)
• Clause 5.1: LWP can only be applied for after exhausting all applicable paid leave entitlements.
• Clause 5.2: LWP REQUIRES approval from BOTH the Department Head AND the HR Director. Manager approval alone is NOT sufficient.
• Clause 5.3: LWP exceeding 30 continuous days REQUIRES approval from the Municipal Commissioner.
• Clause 5.4: LWP periods do NOT count toward seniority, increments, or retirement benefits.

SECTION 6: PUBLIC HOLIDAYS & COMPENSATORY OFF (CLAUSES 6.1 – 6.3)
• Entitled to gazetted public holidays. Work on a holiday earns 1 compensatory off day within 60 days. Compensatory off cannot be encashed.

SECTION 7: LEAVE ENCASHMENT (CLAUSES 7.1 – 7.3)
• Clause 7.1: Annual leave may be encashed only upon retirement or resignation (max 60 days).
• Clause 7.2: Leave encashment during service is NOT PERMITTED under any circumstances.
• Clause 7.3: Sick leave and LWP cannot be encashed under any circumstances.

SECTION 8: GRIEVANCES (CLAUSES 8.1 – 8.2)
• Grievances must be raised in writing with HR within 10 working days of the disputed decision.
