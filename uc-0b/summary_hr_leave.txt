CITY MUNICIPAL CORPORATION - EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY SUMMARY
================================================================================

This high-fidelity summary contains the binding clauses extracted directly from
the Employee Leave Policy document. To ensure absolute compliance, prevent obligation
softening, and avoid dropping critical approval conditions or introducing scope bleed,
each target clause has been quoted verbatim and flagged accordingly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BINDING OBLIGATIONS & CLAUSES INVENTORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[VERBATIM OBLIGATION] Clause 2.3: Employees must submit a leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1.
  - Binding Verb: must (14-day advance notice required)
  - Instrument Required: Form HR-L1

[VERBATIM OBLIGATION] Clause 2.4: Leave applications must receive written approval from the employee's direct manager before the leave commences. Verbal approval is not valid.
  - Binding Verb: must (written approval required before leave commences)
  - Exclusions: Verbal approval is strictly invalid
  - Approver: Direct manager

[VERBATIM OBLIGATION] Clause 2.5: Unapproved absence will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval.
  - Binding Verb: will (unapproved absence recorded as Loss of Pay)
  - Constraint: Applies regardless of subsequent approval

[VERBATIM OBLIGATION] Clause 2.6: Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year. Any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December.
  - Binding Verb: may / are forfeited (maximum 5 carry-forward days)
  - Constraint: Excess days are forfeited on 31 December

[VERBATIM OBLIGATION] Clause 2.7: Carry-forward days must be used within the first quarter (January–March) of the following year or they are forfeited.
  - Binding Verb: must / are forfeited (carry-forward usage timeline)
  - Condition: Must be used within Jan-Mar of the following year

[VERBATIM OBLIGATION] Clause 3.2: Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 48 hours of returning to work.
  - Binding Verb: requires (medical certificate for sick leave)
  - Condition: 3 or more consecutive sick days
  - Authority: Registered medical practitioner
  - Timeline: Submitted within 48 hours of returning to work

[VERBATIM OBLIGATION] Clause 3.4: Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave period requires a medical certificate regardless of duration.
  - Binding Verb: requires (medical certificate regardless of duration)
  - Condition: Sick leave taken immediately before/after public holiday or annual leave

[VERBATIM OBLIGATION] Clause 5.2: LWP requires approval from the Department Head and the HR Director. Manager approval alone is not sufficient.
  - Binding Verb: requires (Leave Without Pay approvals)
  - Approvers: BOTH Department Head AND HR Director (Manager approval alone is not sufficient)

[VERBATIM OBLIGATION] Clause 5.3: LWP exceeding 30 continuous days requires approval from the Municipal Commissioner.
  - Binding Verb: requires (extended Leave Without Pay approval)
  - Condition: LWP exceeding 30 continuous days
  - Approver: Municipal Commissioner

[VERBATIM OBLIGATION] Clause 7.2: Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances.
  - Binding Verb: not permitted (leave encashment prohibition)
  - Constraint: Absolute prohibition during service under any circumstances
