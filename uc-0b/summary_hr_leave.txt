Clause 1.1 (PURPOSE AND SCOPE): Applies to CMC's permanent and contractual employees only.

Clause 1.2 (PURPOSE AND SCOPE): Excludes daily wage workers and consultants — their contracts govern instead.

Clause 2.1 (ANNUAL LEAVE): Permanent employees get 18 paid annual leave days per calendar year.

Clause 2.2 (ANNUAL LEAVE): Annual leave accrues monthly at 1.5 days from joining date.

Clause 2.3 (ANNUAL LEAVE): Must apply at least 14 calendar days ahead, using Form HR-L1.

Clause 2.4 (ANNUAL LEAVE): Requires written approval from the employee's direct manager before leave starts — verbal approval invalid.

Clause 2.5 (ANNUAL LEAVE): Unapproved absence is recorded as Loss of Pay, even if approved later.

Clause 2.6 (ANNUAL LEAVE): Up to 5 unused annual leave days may carry forward to next year; anything above 5 is forfeited on Dec 31.

Clause 2.7 (ANNUAL LEAVE): Carried-forward days must be used by end of Q1 (Jan–Mar) next year or are forfeited.

Clause 3.1 (SICK LEAVE): Employees get 12 paid sick leave days per calendar year.

Clause 3.2 (SICK LEAVE): 3+ consecutive sick days require a registered medical practitioner's certificate, submitted within 48 hours of returning to work.

Clause 3.3 (SICK LEAVE): Sick leave does not carry forward to the next year.

Clause 3.4 (SICK LEAVE): Sick leave taken right before/after a public holiday or annual leave needs a medical certificate regardless of duration.

Clause 4.1 (MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE): Female employees get 26 weeks' paid maternity leave for the first two live births.

Clause 4.2 (MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE): Third or later child: maternity leave is 12 weeks paid.

Clause 4.3 (MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE): Male employees get 5 days' paid paternity leave, to be taken within 30 days of the child's birth.

Clause 4.4 (MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE): [VERBATIM — not summarized] "Paternity leave cannot be split across multiple periods."

Clause 5.1 (LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)): LWP may only be applied for after all paid leave entitlements are exhausted.

Clause 5.2 (LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)): Requires approval from both the Department Head and HR Director — manager approval alone is not enough.

Clause 5.3 (LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)): LWP over 30 continuous days needs Municipal Commissioner approval.

Clause 5.4 (LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)): LWP periods don't count toward seniority, increments, or retirement benefit service.

Clause 6.1 (PUBLIC HOLIDAYS): Employees get all State-Government-declared gazetted public holidays.

Clause 6.2 (PUBLIC HOLIDAYS): Working on a public holiday earns one compensatory off, to be taken within 60 days of that holiday.

Clause 6.3 (PUBLIC HOLIDAYS): [VERBATIM — not summarized] "Compensatory off cannot be encashed."

Clause 7.1 (LEAVE ENCASHMENT): Annual leave is encashable only at retirement or resignation, capped at 60 days.

Clause 7.2 (LEAVE ENCASHMENT): Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances.

Clause 7.3 (LEAVE ENCASHMENT): Sick leave and LWP cannot be encashed under any circumstances.

Clause 8.1 (GRIEVANCES): Leave grievances must go to HR within 10 working days of the disputed decision.

Clause 8.2 (GRIEVANCES): Grievances filed after 10 working days are rejected unless exceptional circumstances are shown in writing.
