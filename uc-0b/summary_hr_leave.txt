Here is the summary of the policy text in exactly 10 points:

1.  This policy governs all leave entitlements for permanent and contractual employees of the City Municipal Corporation (CMC) and does not apply to daily wage workers or consultants.
2.  Each permanent employee is entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accruing at 1.5 days per month from the date of joining.
3.  Annual leave applications must be submitted at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1 AND must receive written approval from the direct manager before the leave commences; verbal approval is not valid. Unapproved absence will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP).
4.  Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year, but these must be used within the first quarter (January–March) or they are forfeited. Any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December.
5.  Each employee is entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days OR sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave period requires a medical certificate. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.
6.  Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two live births, and 12 weeks paid for a third or subsequent child. Male employees are entitled to 5 days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 30 days of the child's birth, and cannot be split.
7.  Leave Without Pay (LWP) may only be applied for after exhausting all applicable paid leave entitlements. LWP requires approval from the Department Head AND the HR Director. LWP exceeding 30 continuous days requires approval from the Municipal Commissioner. Periods of LWP do not count toward service for seniority, increments, or retirement benefits.
8.  Employees are entitled to all gazetted public holidays. If required to work on a public holiday, employees are entitled to one compensatory off day, to be taken within 60 days, which cannot be encashed.
9.  Annual leave may be encashed only at the time of retirement OR resignation, subject to a maximum of 60 days. Leave encashment during service, sick leave, and LWP encashment are not permitted under any circumstances.
10. Leave-related grievances must be raised with the HR Department within 10 working days of the disputed decision; grievances raised after 10 working days will not be considered unless exceptional circumstances are demonstrated in writing.
