SUMMARY OF EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY (HR-POL-001)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Effective Date: 1 April 2024 | Version: 2.3
Scope: Permanent and contractual employees of City Municipal Corporation (CMC).
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

### 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
- Clause 1.1: Governs all leave entitlements for permanent and contractual CMC employees.
- Clause 1.2: Does NOT apply to daily wage workers or consultants (governed by separate contracts).

### 2. ANNUAL LEAVE
- Clause 2.1: Permanent employees are entitled to 18 days paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Clause 2.2: Annual leave accrues at 1.5 days per month from joining date.
- Clause 2.3 [CRITICAL]: Employees MUST submit leave applications at least 14 calendar days in advance via Form HR-L1.
- Clause 2.4 [CRITICAL]: Written approval from direct manager is strictly required before leave commences; verbal approval is NOT valid.
- Clause 2.5 [CRITICAL]: Unapproved absence will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of any subsequent approval.
- Clause 2.6 [CRITICAL]: Maximum 5 unused annual leave days may be carried forward; any days exceeding 5 are forfeited on 31 December.
- Clause 2.7 [CRITICAL]: Carry-forward leave MUST be used in Q1 (January–March) or it is forfeited.

### 3. SICK LEAVE
- Clause 3.1: Entitlement of 12 days paid sick leave per calendar year.
- Clause 3.2 [CRITICAL]: Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner within 48 hours of return.
- Clause 3.3: Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the following year.
- Clause 3.4 [CRITICAL]: Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday/annual leave requires a medical certificate regardless of duration.

### 4. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
- Clause 4.1: Female employees receive 26 weeks paid maternity leave for the first two live births.
- Clause 4.2: For a third or subsequent child, maternity leave is 12 weeks paid.
- Clause 4.3: Male employees receive 5 days paid paternity leave, to be taken within 30 days of birth.
- Clause 4.4: Paternity leave cannot be split across multiple periods.

### 5. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)
- Clause 5.1: Leave Without Pay (LWP) application permitted only after exhausting all applicable paid leave.
- Clause 5.2 [CRITICAL - DUAL APPROVAL]: LWP strictly requires approval from BOTH the Department Head AND the HR Director; manager approval alone is NOT sufficient.
- Clause 5.3 [CRITICAL - COMMISSIONER APPROVAL]: LWP exceeding 30 continuous days requires approval from the Municipal Commissioner.
- Clause 5.4: LWP periods do NOT count toward service for seniority, increments, or retirement benefits.

### 6. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
- Clause 6.1: Employees are entitled to all State Government gazetted public holidays.
- Clause 6.2: Work required on a public holiday entitles employee to one compensatory off day within 60 days.
- Clause 6.3: Compensatory off cannot be encashed.

### 7. LEAVE ENCASHMENT
- Clause 7.1: Annual leave encashment is permitted only at retirement or resignation, capped at 60 days.
- Clause 7.2 [CRITICAL - ABSOLUTE PROHIBITION]: Leave encashment during active service is NOT permitted under any circumstances.
- Clause 7.3: Sick leave and LWP cannot be encashed under any circumstances.

### 8. GRIEVANCES
- Clause 8.1: Leave grievances must be submitted to HR Department within 10 working days of the disputed decision.
- Clause 8.2: Grievances submitted after 10 working days will not be considered unless exceptional circumstances are demonstrated in writing.
