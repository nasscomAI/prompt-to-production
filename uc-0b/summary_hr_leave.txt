           [2.2] [2.2] 2.2 Annual leave accrues at 1.5 days per month from the date of joining.

           [2.3] [2.3] 2.3 Employees must submit a leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1.
             Binding obligation: 'must'

           [2.4] [2.4] 2.4 Leave applications must receive written approval from the employee's direct manager before the leave commences. Verbal approval is not valid.
             Binding obligation: 'must'

           [2.5] [2.5] 2.5 Unapproved absence will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval.
             Binding obligation: 'will'

           [2.6] [2.6] 2.6 Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year. Any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December.
             Binding obligation: 'may'

           [2.7] [2.7] 2.7 Carry-forward days must be used within the first quarter (January–March) of the following year or they are forfeited. calendar year.
