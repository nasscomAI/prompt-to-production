CITY MUNICIPAL CORPORATION — HR LEAVE POLICY EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Document Reference: HR-POL-001 | Version: 2.3 | Effective: 1 April 2024
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
- [Clause 1.1] Governs all leave entitlements for permanent and contractual employees of the City Municipal Corporation (CMC).
- [Clause 1.2] Does not apply to daily wage workers or consultants (governed by respective contracts).

2. ANNUAL LEAVE
- [Clause 2.1] Entitlement: 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year for permanent employees.
- [Clause 2.2] Accrual: Accrues at 1.5 days per month from date of joining.
- [Clause 2.3] Notice Requirement: Employees MUST submit a leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1.
- [Clause 2.4] Approval Requirement: MUST receive WRITTEN approval from direct manager before leave commences. Verbal approval is NOT valid.
- [Clause 2.5] Unapproved Absence: Recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval.
- [Clause 2.6] Carry-Forward Limit: Maximum 5 unused annual leave days may be carried forward; days above 5 ARE FORFEITED on 31 December.
- [Clause 2.7] Carry-Forward Expiry: Carry-forward days MUST be used within Q1 (January–March) of following year or they ARE FORFEITED.

3. SICK LEAVE
- [Clause 3.1] Entitlement: 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
- [Clause 3.2] Medical Certificate (3+ Days): Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days REQUIRES a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 48 hours of returning to work.
- [Clause 3.3] Carry-Forward: Cannot be carried forward to following year.
- [Clause 3.4] Medical Certificate (Adjacent to Holiday/Leave): Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave period REQUIRES a medical certificate regardless of duration.

4. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
- [Clause 4.1] Maternity Leave (1st/2nd Birth): Female employees entitled to 26 weeks paid maternity leave for first two live births.
- [Clause 4.2] Maternity Leave (Subsequent): 12 weeks paid for 3rd or subsequent child.
- [Clause 4.3] Paternity Leave: Male employees entitled to 5 days paid paternity leave within 30 days of birth.
- [Clause 4.4] Paternity Leave Splitting: Cannot be split across multiple periods.

5. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)
- [Clause 5.1] Prerequisite: Applicable only after exhausting all paid leave entitlements.
- [Clause 5.2] Dual Approval Requirement: REQUIRES approval from BOTH the Department Head AND the HR Director. Manager approval alone is NOT sufficient.
- [Clause 5.3] Extended LWP (>30 Days): LWP exceeding 30 continuous days REQUIRES approval from the Municipal Commissioner.
- [Clause 5.4] Service Impact: LWP periods do NOT count toward service for seniority, increments, or retirement benefits.

6. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
- [Clause 6.1] Entitlement: All state government gazetted public holidays.
- [Clause 6.2] Holiday Work: Compensatory off day granted within 60 days of holiday worked.
- [Clause 6.3] Encashment: Compensatory off cannot be encashed.

7. LEAVE ENCASHMENT
- [Clause 7.1] Permissible Encashment: Annual leave encashable ONLY upon retirement or resignation (max 60 days).
- [Clause 7.2] Service Encashment Prohibition: Leave encashment during service is NOT PERMITTED under any circumstances.
- [Clause 7.3] Other Leave Encashment: Sick leave and LWP cannot be encashed under any circumstances.

8. GRIEVANCES
- [Clause 8.1] Timeline: Leave-related grievances MUST be raised with HR within 10 working days of disputed decision.
- [Clause 8.2] Late Submissions: Grievances after 10 working days WILL NOT be considered unless exceptional circumstances are demonstrated in writing.
