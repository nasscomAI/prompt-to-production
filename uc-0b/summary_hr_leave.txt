HR LEAVE POLICY SUMMARY (COMPLIANT)
====================================

Annual Leave:
- [2.3] Employees must submit a leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance.
- [2.4] Written approval from the direct manager must be received before leave commences. Verbal approval is not valid.
- [2.5] Unapproved absence will be recorded as LOP regardless of subsequent approval.
- [2.6] Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days; any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December.
- [2.7] Carry-forward days must be used January–March of the following year or they are forfeited.

Sick Leave:
- [3.2] Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate within 48 hours.
- [3.4] Sick leave immediately before/after a holiday or annual leave requires a medical certificate regardless of duration.

Leave Without Pay (LWP):
- [5.2] LWP requires approval from both the Department Head AND the HR Director.
- [5.3] LWP exceeding 30 continuous days requires approval from the Municipal Commissioner.

Leave Encashment:
- [7.2] Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances.
