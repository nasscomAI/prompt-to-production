# Policy Summary — HR Leave Policy

- **Clause 2.3**: 14-day advance notice required via Form HR-L1.
- **Clause 2.4**: Written approval required before leave commences; verbal approval is NOT valid.
- **Clause 2.5**: Unapproved absence results in Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval.
- **Clause 2.6**: Max 5 days carry-forward; days above 5 are forfeited on 31 Dec.
- **Clause 2.7**: Carry-forward days must be used in Q1 (Jan–Mar) or they are forfeited.
- **Clause 3.2**: 3+ consecutive sick days requires medical cert within 48hrs of return.
- **Clause 3.4**: Sick leave before/after holiday/annual leave requires cert regardless of duration.
- **Clause 5.2**: Requires approval from BOTH Department Head and HR Director. Manager approval alone is not sufficient.
- **Clause 5.3**: LWP exceeding 30 continuous days requires Municipal Commissioner approval.
- **Clause 7.2**: Leave encashment DURING service is not permitted under any circumstances.
