2.3 | 14-day advance notice required
    Source text: Employees must submit a leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1.
    Binding verb: [must]
2.4 | Written approval required before leave commences; verbal invalid
    Source text: Leave applications must receive written approval from the employee's direct manager before the leave commences. Verbal approval is not valid.
    Binding verb: [must]
2.5 | Unapproved absence = Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of late approvals
    Source text: Unapproved absence will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval.
    Binding verb: [will]
2.6 | Max 5 days carry-forward; anything above forfeited on Dec 31
    Source text: Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year. Any days above 5 are forfeited on 31 December.
    Binding verb: [are forfeited]
2.7 | Carry-forward days must be spent Jan–Mar or forfeited
    Source text: Carry-forward days must be used within the first quarter (January–March) of the following year or they are forfeited.
    Binding verb: [are forfeited]
3.2 | 3+ consecutive sick days requires medical cert within 48 hours
    Source text: Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 48 hours of returning to work.
    Binding verb: [requires]
3.4 | Sick leave adjacent to a holiday requires cert regardless of length
    Source text: Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave period requires a medical certificate regardless of duration.
    Binding verb: [requires]
5.2 | LWP requires BOTH Department Head AND HR Director approval
    Source text: LWP requires approval from the Department Head and the HR Director. Manager approval alone is not sufficient.
    Binding verb: [requires]
5.3 | LWP exceeding 30 days requires Municipal Commissioner approval
    Source text: LWP exceeding 30 continuous days requires approval from the Municipal Commissioner.
    Binding verb: [requires]
7.2 | Leave encashment during active service is not permitted under any circumstances
    Source text: Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances.
    Binding verb: [is not permitted]

Additional clauses
--------------------
  1.1: This policy governs all leave entitlements for permanent and contractual employees of the City Municipal Corporation (CMC).
  1.2: This policy does not apply to daily wage workers or consultants. Those categories are governed by their respective contracts.
  2.1: Each permanent employee is entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
  2.2: Annual leave accrues at 1.5 days per month from the date of joining.
  3.1: Each employee is entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
  3.3: Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the following year. [cannot]
  4.1: Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two live births.
  4.2: For a third or subsequent child, maternity leave is 12 weeks paid.
  4.3: Male employees are entitled to 5 days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 30 days of the child's birth.
  4.4: Paternity leave cannot be split across multiple periods. [cannot]
  5.1: An employee may apply for Leave Without Pay only after exhausting all applicable paid leave entitlements. [may]
  5.4: Periods of LWP do not count toward service for the purposes of seniority, increments, or retirement benefits.
  6.1: Employees are entitled to all gazetted public holidays as declared by the State Government each year.
  6.2: If an employee is required to work on a public holiday, they are entitled to one compensatory off day, to be taken within 60 days of the holiday worked. [require]
  6.3: Compensatory off cannot be encashed. [cannot]
  7.1: Annual leave may be encashed only at the time of retirement or resignation, subject to a maximum of 60 days. [may]
  7.3: Sick leave and LWP cannot be encashed under any circumstances. [cannot]
  8.1: Leave-related grievances must be raised with the HR Department within 10 working days of the disputed decision. [must]
  8.2: Grievances raised after 10 working days will not be considered unless exceptional circumstances are demonstrated in writing. [will]
