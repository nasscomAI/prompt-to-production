1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
  - Clause 1.1: This policy governs all leave entitlements for permanent and contractual employees of the City Municipal Corporation (CMC).
  - Clause 1.2 [CRITICAL]: This policy does NOT apply to daily wage workers or consultants. Those categories are governed by their respective contracts.

2. ANNUAL LEAVE
  - Clauses 2.1, 2.2: Each permanent employee gets 18 days of paid annual leave /year; Annual leave accrues at 1.5 days /month from joining.
  - Clause 2.3 [CRITICAL]: Employees MUST submit a leave application ≥ 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1.
  - Clause 2.4 [CRITICAL]: Leave applications MUST receive written approval from the employee's direct manager before the leave commences. Verbal approval is NOT VALID.
  - Clause 2.5 [CRITICAL]: Unapproved absence WILL be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval.
  - Clause 2.6 [CRITICAL]: Employees may carry forward max 5 unused annual leave days to next year. Any days above 5 ARE FORFEITED on 31 December.
  - Clause 2.7 [CRITICAL]: Carry-forward days MUST be used in Jan–Mar next year or they ARE FORFEITED.

3. SICK LEAVE
  - Clause 3.1: Each employee gets 12 days of paid sick leave /year.
  - Clause 3.2 [CRITICAL]: Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days REQUIRES a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 48 hours of return.
  - Clause 3.3 [CRITICAL]: Sick leave CANNOT be carried forward to the following year.
  - Clause 3.4 [CRITICAL]: Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave period REQUIRES a medical certificate regardless of duration.

4. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
  - Clauses 4.1, 4.2: Female employees get 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two live births; For a third or subsequent child, maternity leave is 12 weeks paid.
  - Clause 4.3 [CRITICAL]: Male employees get 5 days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 30 days of the child's birth.
  - Clause 4.4 [CRITICAL]: Paternity leave CANNOT be split across multiple periods.

5. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)
  - Clause 5.1 [CRITICAL]: An employee may apply for Leave Without Pay ONLY after exhausting all applicable paid leave entitlements.
  - Clause 5.2 [CRITICAL]: LWP REQUIRES approval from the Department Head and the HR Director. Manager approval alone is NOT SUFFICIENT.
  - Clause 5.3 [CRITICAL]: LWP exceeding 30 continuous days REQUIRES approval from the Municipal Commissioner.
  - Clause 5.4 [CRITICAL]: Periods of LWP do NOT count toward service for the purposes of seniority, increments, or retirement benefits.

6. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
  - Clause 6.1: Employees get all gazetted public holidays as declared by the State Government each year.
  - Clause 6.2 [CRITICAL]: If an employee is REQUIRED to work on a public holiday, they get one compensatory off day, to be taken within 60 days of the holiday worked.
  - Clause 6.3 [CRITICAL]: Compensatory off CANNOT be encashed.

7. LEAVE ENCASHMENT
  - Clause 7.1 [CRITICAL]: Annual leave may be encashed ONLY at the time of retirement or resignation, subject to max 60 days.
  - Clause 7.2 [CRITICAL]: Leave encashment during service is NOT PERMITTED under any circumstances.
  - Clause 7.3 [CRITICAL]: Sick leave and LWP CANNOT be encashed under any circumstances.

8. GRIEVANCES
  - Clause 8.1 [CRITICAL]: Leave-related grievances MUST be raised with the HR Department within 10 working days of the disputed decision.
  - Clause 8.2 [CRITICAL]: Grievances raised after 10 working days WILL NOT be considered unless exceptional circumstances are demonstrated in writing.
