# HR Leave Policy Summary


**Section 1**
- **1.1**: Applies to permanent and contractual employees of the City Municipal Corporation.
- **1.2**: Excludes daily wage workers and consultants (governed by own contracts).

**Section 2**
- **2.1**: Permanent employees get 18 paid annual leave days/year.
- **2.2**: Accrues at 1.5 days/month from joining date.
- **2.3**: 14 calendar days advance notice required via Form HR-L1.
- **2.4**: [FLAGGED FOR EXACT QUOTE] "Leave applications must receive written approval from the employee's direct manager before the leave commences. Verbal approval is not valid."
- **2.5**: [FLAGGED FOR EXACT QUOTE] "Unapproved absence will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval."
- **2.6**: Max 5 unused annual leave days can carry forward; excess forfeited on 31 Dec.
- **2.7**: Carry-forward days must be used by March 31 or forfeited.

**Section 3**
- **3.1**: 12 paid sick leave days per calendar year.
- **3.2**: [FLAGGED FOR EXACT QUOTE] "Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 48 hours of returning to work."
- **3.3**: Sick leave cannot be carried forward.
- **3.4**: Medical cert required for sick leave before/after public holiday/annual leave.

**Section 4**
- **4.1**: Female employees get 26 weeks paid maternity leave for first two live births.
- **4.2**: 12 weeks paid maternity leave for third or subsequent child.
- **4.3**: Male employees get 5 days paid paternity leave within 30 days of birth.
- **4.4**: Paternity leave cannot be split.

**Section 5**
- **5.1**: LWP only applicable after exhausting all paid leave.
- **5.2**: [FLAGGED FOR EXACT QUOTE] "LWP requires approval from the Department Head and the HR Director. Manager approval alone is not sufficient."
- **5.3**: LWP > 30 continuous days requires Municipal Commissioner approval.
- **5.4**: LWP does not count toward service for seniority, increments, or retirement.

**Section 6**
- **6.1**: Entitled to all State Government gazetted public holidays.
- **6.2**: Working on public holiday entitles to 1 compensatory off day within 60 days.
- **6.3**: Compensatory off cannot be encashed.

**Section 7**
- **7.1**: Annual leave encashment only at retirement/resignation (max 60 days).
- **7.2**: [FLAGGED FOR EXACT QUOTE] "Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances."
- **7.3**: Sick leave and LWP cannot be encashed.

**Section 8**
- **8.1**: Leave grievances must be raised with HR within 10 working days.
- **8.2**: Grievances after 10 days ignored unless exceptional circumstances demonstrated in writing.
