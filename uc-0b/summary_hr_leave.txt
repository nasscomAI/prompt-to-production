EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY SUMMARY (HR-POL-001)
City Municipal Corporation | Effective: 1 April 2024
============================================================

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
- [Clause 1.1] Governs all leave entitlements for permanent and contractual employees of CMC.
- [Clause 1.2] Does not apply to daily wage workers or consultants (governed by separate contracts).

2. ANNUAL LEAVE
- [Clause 2.1] Entitlement: 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year for permanent employees.
- [Clause 2.2] Accrual rate: 1.5 days per month from date of joining.
- [Clause 2.3] Advance Notice: Employees must submit leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1.
- [Clause 2.4] Written Approval: Must receive written approval from direct manager before leave commences; verbal approval is not valid.
- [Clause 2.5] Unapproved Absence: Will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of any subsequent approval.
- [Clause 2.6] Carry-Forward Limit: Maximum 5 unused days may be carried forward; days in excess of 5 are forfeited on 31 December.
- [Clause 2.7] Carry-Forward Expiry: Carried forward days must be utilized within Q1 (January–March) or they are forfeited.

3. SICK LEAVE
- [Clause 3.1] Entitlement: 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
- [Clause 3.2] Medical Certificate (3+ Days): Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 48 hours of returning to work.
- [Clause 3.3] Carry-Forward: Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the following year.
- [Clause 3.4] Adjacent to Holidays: Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave requires a medical certificate regardless of duration.

4. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
- [Clause 4.1] Maternity (First 2 Live Births): 26 weeks paid maternity leave.
- [Clause 4.2] Maternity (3rd+ Child): 12 weeks paid maternity leave.
- [Clause 4.3] Paternity: 5 days paid paternity leave for male employees, must be taken within 30 days of child's birth.
- [Clause 4.4] Paternity Split Rule: Cannot be split across multiple periods.

5. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)
- [Clause 5.1] Eligibility: Permitted only after exhausting all applicable paid leave entitlements.
- [Clause 5.2] Approval Authority: Requires approval from BOTH the Department Head and the HR Director; direct manager approval alone is NOT sufficient.
- [Clause 5.3] Extended LWP (>30 Days): LWP exceeding 30 continuous days requires approval from the Municipal Commissioner.
- [Clause 5.4] Service Impact: LWP periods do not count toward service for seniority, increments, or retirement benefits.

6. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
- [Clause 6.1] Entitled to all gazetted public holidays declared by the State Government.
- [Clause 6.2] Work on Public Holiday: 1 compensatory off day granted, to be used within 60 days of the worked holiday.
- [Clause 6.3] Encashment: Compensatory off cannot be encashed.

7. LEAVE ENCASHMENT
- [Clause 7.1] Permitted only at retirement or resignation, capped at a maximum of 60 days.
- [Clause 7.2] During Service: Leave encashment during service is NOT permitted under any circumstances.
- [Clause 7.3] Sick Leave & LWP: Cannot be encashed under any circumstances.

8. GRIEVANCES
- [Clause 8.1] Must be submitted to the HR Department within 10 working days of the disputed decision.
- [Clause 8.2] Late submissions (>10 working days) will not be considered unless exceptional circumstances are demonstrated in writing.
