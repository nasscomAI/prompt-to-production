# Compressed Policy Summary

**Clause 1.1** — Governs reimbursement of CMC employees' expenses incurred during official duties.

**Clause 1.2** — [VERBATIM — not summarized] "Personal expenses are not reimbursable under any circumstances."

**Clause 1.3** — Claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense; claims filed later will not be processed.

**Clause 2.1** — Local travel reimbursed at actual cost (public transport) or Rs 4/km (personal vehicle); receipts required for claims over Rs 200.

**Clause 2.2** — Outstation travel requires prior approval via Form FIN-T1 before travel starts; unapproved travel is not reimbursable.

**Clause 2.3** — Air travel allowed only for journeys over 500 km; economy class is mandatory, business class is not reimbursable.

**Clause 2.4** — Hotel costs reimbursable up to Rs 3,500/night in Grade A cities and Rs 2,500/night elsewhere.

**Clause 2.5** — Daily allowance for outstation travel is Rs 750/day, covering meals and incidentals; no separate meal receipts needed if DA is claimed.

**Clause 2.6** — If claiming actual meal costs instead of DA, receipts are mandatory and the total must not exceed Rs 750/day; DA and meal receipts cannot both be claimed for the same day.

**Clause 3.1** — Employees approved for permanent work-from-home get a one-time Rs 8,000 home office equipment allowance.

**Clause 3.2** — Allowance covers only: desk, chair, monitor, keyboard, mouse, and networking equipment.

**Clause 3.3** — Allowance excludes: personal computers, laptops, smartphones, printers, and air conditioning equipment.

**Clause 3.4** — Claims need original receipts, submitted within 60 days of the Department Head's written approval of the WFH arrangement.

**Clause 3.5** — Employees on temporary or partial work-from-home arrangements are not eligible for this allowance.

**Clause 4.1** — Training costs are reimbursable only if pre-approved by the Department Head via Form FIN-TR1.

**Clause 4.2** — Course fees reimbursable up to Rs 15,000 per employee per financial year.

**Clause 4.3** — Certification exam fees reimbursable once per attempt per certification, up to Rs 5,000.

**Clause 4.4** — Employees leaving CMC within 12 months of a reimbursed training expense must repay 100% of it; leaving between 12–24 months requires repaying 50%.

**Clause 5.1** — Grade C and above employees get a Rs 500/month mobile phone reimbursement.

**Clause 5.2** — Grade B and above employees get a Rs 800/month internet reimbursement, only for approved work-from-home arrangements.

**Clause 5.3** — These reimbursements require the original bill each month; estimated or self-declared amounts are not accepted.

**Clause 6.1** — All claims must be submitted via the CMC employee portal using Form FIN-EXP1.

**Clause 6.2** — Original receipts must be attached; photocopies or digital screenshots are accepted only if the vendor issues no physical receipt.

**Clause 6.3** — Claims are processed within 15 working days of submission.

**Clause 6.4** — Disputed claims must be raised with the Finance Department within 10 working days of receiving the reimbursement decision.
