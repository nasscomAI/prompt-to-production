# CMC Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy — Summary

**Document:** FIN-POL-007, v3.1, effective April 1, 2024

## General Rules
- Only official expenses are reimbursable (no personal expenses)
- Claims must be submitted within **30 days** of expense; late claims are rejected

## Travel
- **Local travel:** Actual cost (public transport) or Rs 4/km (personal vehicle); receipts required above Rs 200
- **Outstation travel:** Requires pre-approval via Form FIN-T1
- **Air travel:** Only for trips >500 km; economy class only
- **Hotels:** Up to Rs 3,500/night (Grade A cities) or Rs 2,500/night (other cities)
- **Daily Allowance:** Rs 750/day covering meals/incidentals — either claim DA OR itemized meal receipts (capped at Rs 750/day), not both

## Work-From-Home Equipment
- One-time Rs 8,000 allowance for **permanent** WFH employees only
- Covers desk, chair, monitor, keyboard, mouse, networking gear (not laptops/phones/printers/AC)
- Must submit receipts within 60 days of written WFH approval

## Training & Development
- Requires pre-approval via Form FIN-TR1
- Course fees: up to Rs 15,000/year/employee
- Certification exam fees: up to Rs 5,000 per attempt
- **Clawback:** Must repay 100% if resigning within 12 months of training; 50% if within 12–24 months

## Mobile & Internet
- Mobile: Rs 500/month for Grade C and above
- Internet: Rs 800/month for Grade B and above (approved WFH only)
- Original bills required monthly (no estimates/self-declarations)

## Submission Process
- Submit via employee portal using Form FIN-EXP1
- Original receipts required (photocopies/screenshots only if vendor doesn't issue physical receipts)
- Processing time: 15 working days
- Disputes must be raised within 10 working days of decision
