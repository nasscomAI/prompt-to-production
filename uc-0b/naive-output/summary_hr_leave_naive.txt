# Summary: CMC Employee Leave Policy (HR-POL-001, v2.3)

## Scope
Applies to permanent and contractual employees only — excludes daily wage workers and consultants (governed by separate contracts).

## Key Leave Categories

**Annual Leave**
- 18 days/year, accruing at 1.5 days/month
- Requires 14 days' advance notice (Form HR-L1) and **written** manager approval (verbal approval invalid)
- Unapproved absence = Loss of Pay, even if approved later
- Max 5 days carry-forward, must be used by Q1 of next year or forfeited

**Sick Leave**
- 12 days/year, no carry-forward
- Medical certificate required for 3+ consecutive days (submit within 48 hrs of return)
- Certificate also required regardless of duration if taken adjacent to holidays/annual leave

**Maternity/Paternity Leave**
- Maternity: 26 weeks (first two children), 12 weeks (third+ child)
- Paternity: 5 days, must be taken within 30 days of birth, cannot be split

**Leave Without Pay (LWP)**
- Only after exhausting paid leave
- Needs **both** Department Head and HR Director approval; 30+ continuous days needs Municipal Commissioner approval
- Does not count toward seniority, increments, or retirement benefits

**Public Holidays**
- Gazetted holidays granted; working on one earns a compensatory off (use within 60 days, non-encashable)

**Leave Encashment**
- Annual leave encashable only at retirement/resignation (max 60 days)
- No mid-service encashment; sick leave and LWP never encashable

## Grievances
Must be filed within 10 working days of the disputed decision; late grievances rejected unless exceptional circumstances are proven in writing.
