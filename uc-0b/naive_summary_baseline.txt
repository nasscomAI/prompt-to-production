- Purpose And Scope: This policy governs all leave entitlements for permanent and contractual employees of the City Municipal Corporation (CMC).
- Annual Leave: Each permanent employee is entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Sick Leave: Each employee is entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
- Maternity And Paternity Leave: Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two live births.
- Leave Without Pay (Lwp): An employee may apply for Leave Without Pay only after exhausting all applicable paid leave entitlements.
- Public Holidays: Employees are entitled to all gazetted public holidays as declared by the State Government each year.
- Leave Encashment: Annual leave may be encashed only at the time of retirement or resignation, subject to a maximum of 60 days.
- Grievances: Leave-related grievances should be raised with the HR Department within 10 working days of the disputed decision.

Employees are generally expected to follow these rules as is standard practice in most cases.
