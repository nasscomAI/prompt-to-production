EMPLOYEE EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY — SUMMARY

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
  - Clause 1.1: This policy governs reimbursement of expenses incurred by CMC employees on official duties.
  - Clause 1.2 [CRITICAL]: Personal expenses are NOT REIMBURSABLE under any circumstances.
  - Clause 1.3 [CRITICAL]: All claims MUST be submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 30 days WILL NOT be processed.

2. TRAVEL REIMBURSEMENT
  - Clause 2.1 [CRITICAL]: Local travel (within city limits) is reimbursable at actual cost for public transport or at Rs 4 per km for personal vehicle use. Receipts are REQUIRED for all claims above Rs 200.
  - Clause 2.2 [CRITICAL]: Outstation travel MUST be pre-approved using Form FIN-T1 before travel commences. Travel without prior approval is NOT REIMBURSABLE.
  - Clause 2.3 [CRITICAL]: Air travel is permitted for journeys exceeding 500 km ONLY. Economy class is MANDATORY. Business class is NOT REIMBURSABLE.
  - Clause 2.4 [CRITICAL]: Hotel accommodation for outstation travel is reimbursable ≤ Rs 3,500 /night for Grade A cities and Rs 2,500 /night for other locations.
  - Clause 2.5 [CRITICAL]: Daily allowance (DA) for outstation travel is Rs 750 /day. DA covers meals and incidentals. No separate meal receipts are REQUIRED if DA is claimed.
  - Clause 2.6 [CRITICAL]: If actual meal expenses are claimed instead of DA, receipts are MANDATORY and the combined meal claim MUST NOT exceed Rs 750 /day. DA and meal receipts CANNOT be claimed simultaneously for the same day.

3. WORK FROM HOME EQUIPMENT
  - Clause 3.1: Employees approved for permanent work-from-home arrangements get a one-time home office equipment allowance of Rs 8,000.
  - Clause 3.2 [CRITICAL]: The allowance covers: desk, chair, monitor, keyboard, mouse, and networking equipment ONLY.
  - Clause 3.3 [CRITICAL]: The allowance does NOT cover: personal computers, laptops, smartphones, printers, or air conditioning equipment.
  - Clause 3.4 [CRITICAL]: Claims MUST be submitted with original receipts within 60 days of the work-from-home arrangement being approved in writing by the Department Head.
  - Clause 3.5 [CRITICAL]: Employees on temporary or partial work-from-home arrangements are NOT ELIGIBLE for this allowance.

4. TRAINING AND PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
  - Clauses 4.2, 4.3: Course fees are reimbursable ≤ Rs 15,000 /FY per employee; Exam fees for professional certifications are reimbursable once per attempt per certification, ≤ Rs 5,000.
  - Clause 4.1 [CRITICAL]: Training expenses are reimbursable ONLY if the training was pre-approved by the Department Head using Form FIN-TR1.
  - Clause 4.4 [CRITICAL]: If an employee leaves CMC within 12 months of a reimbursed training expense, they MUST repay 100% of the reimbursed amount. If they leave between 12 and 24 months, they MUST repay 50%.

5. MOBILE PHONE AND INTERNET
  - Clause 5.1: Employees in Grade C and above get a monthly mobile phone reimbursement of Rs 500.
  - Clause 5.2 [CRITICAL]: Employees in Grade B and above get a monthly internet reimbursement of Rs 800 for approved work-from-home arrangements ONLY.
  - Clause 5.3 [CRITICAL]: Reimbursements under this section REQUIRE submission of the original bill each month. Estimated or self-declared amounts are NOT ACCEPTED.

6. SUBMISSION PROCESS
  - Clause 6.3: Claims are processed within 15 working days of submission.
  - Clause 6.1 [CRITICAL]: All reimbursement claims MUST be submitted via the CMC employee portal using Form FIN-EXP1.
  - Clause 6.2 [CRITICAL]: Original receipts MUST be attached. Photocopies and screenshots of digital receipts are accepted ONLY if the vendor does NOT issue physical receipts.
  - Clause 6.4 [CRITICAL]: Disputed claims MUST be raised with the Finance Department within 10 working days of receiving the reimbursement decision.
